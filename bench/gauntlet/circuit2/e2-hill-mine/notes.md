## Defect Observations
- **Baron not killed**: The Baron railcar arrives at wave 12 with ~756 effective HP, but the hero's basic auto-attacks (spark rig, ~24 DPS) are insufficient to kill all 3 baron entities before the wave-18 ceiling. The PressureArsenalSystem (boiler_lance, pressure_mortar, sky_rocket_battery) is not wired into the headless sim (gr-sim), so the pressure generated by boiler houses is not converted into weapon damage.
- **Boiler house building**: Boiler houses CAN be built (2 of 3 maximum, due to gold constraints from 2 active seams each with 40g capacity). Build positions must be offset from the boiler-house-site landmark blocker at (0,12) with radius 2.687; valid positions include (-4,12), (4,12), (0,16), (0,8).
- **Wave ceiling behavior**: Correctly ends the run with `endReason: "wave-ceiling"` at wave 18 (wave 12 secure point + 6 grace waves).
- **Seam activation**: 2 of 5 seams are initially active (seam-1: -30,25 and seam-2: -10,29), each with 30-40g capacity. Seams 3-5 at (-30,39) and (18,25)/(26,39) are inactive initially.
- **Rejection detail clarity**: Build rejections include `detail` causes (`collision` for landmark blocker at (0,12)), confirming the new detail rejection mechanism works correctly.
