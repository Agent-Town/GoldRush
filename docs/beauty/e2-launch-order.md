# E2 BEAUTY NIGHT — LAUNCH ORDER
Authored 2026-08-03 by the E2 art-direction session (branch `beauty2/e2-director`); the four briefs live beside this file.

**Hill Mine first, then Trestle, then Pressure Garden, then Incline.** The Hill Mine is the era's poster and the unlock chain's front door — every player's E2 hours start there, so beauty-per-player-hour concentrates on it — and it is the only map that touches every E2 surface at once (gallery water, terraces, rails, steam anchors, escort cart, railcar boss), which means its shift builds all the shared machinery the other three briefs consume as per-contract config: the water-dressing table + `SCULPT_WATER_CONTRACTS` entries, the E2 rows in `LANDMARK_EMISSIVE`/paint-tint, the steam-anchor puff field, the tinted-mote entry, and above all the `carry_forward_mount_records()` port into `build_e2_contract_terrains.py` that de-arms the landmark-deletion trap for all four maps before anyone repaints an atlas (exactly how the Claim's shift seeded E1's round). The Trestle goes second because it reuses that machinery unchanged and buys the single most dramatic frame in the epoch (the span over real water) for one config entry plus paint; the Pressure Garden third adds only the heat/pressure state reads on top of shipped dry-gulch shimmer machinery; the Incline goes last not because it matters least — its lower yard is the emptiest frame in E2 — but because it is the one map needing a mechanism extension (the two-ford skim in `mountSculptWater`) and it inherits everything else from the three shifts before it, so its shift stays small enough to also choreograph the era's closing frame (the graduation shot: upper cart cresting in steam, lower railcar cooling in the cut).
