## 2. THE STORY (authority: lore/story-arc.md + specs/epoch-saga/*.md — bundles are design-locked)
Ten eras, one question: **"What is a claim?"** E1 answers *gold*; every era re-answers (steam=power, grid=night, motors=distance, sea=depth, atom=home, signal=voice, orbit=sky) until E9 answers *the ground itself* and E10 answers *what you hand on*. **Title thesis: the gold rush was never about the gold.**
- **Three braided arcs**: the HERO (a young woman miner — stakes the claim E1, elder stateswoman E8, hands the Charter Press to the children E10; NEVER called "the Prospector"); the PROSPECTOR (the agent — deputy → present where agents begin, E6 → the Ark's eldest voice); the GENERATIONS (youngsters grow; the moon-born child of E8 — first person never to have seen the river — grows to swim E9's first canal: the saga's emotional spine).
- **The BARON** (pride-wound canon, owner-ruled): pronounced the valley SPENT on his own maps; every era proves him wrong in a new science, in public; he returns to be RIGHT, not rich. Headlines E1/E2 + roughly every second era + the finale; his defeats always hand his machines to the town ("pride's tuition"). Each era also has its own component boss (Railcar, Dynamo Crawler, Land-Yacht, Dredge Queen, Homemaker-9000, the Echo, Salvage King's Claw, Old Digger, the Quiet).
- **The landing**: E8 orbital (the ache: the moon-born child) → E9 Red Fields, ANOTHER world (the heart-era: terraform, canals, green = E1's exact riverbank swatch, "what you shape STAYS"; the owner's own reset-sadness is this era's target emotion) → E10 the Ark (every era one deck; the Static = entropy, "the only enemy that was never greed"; final contracts PRESERVE; the Charter Press — the machine that builds worlds — handed to the children at a child-height lever; credits roll into the family's own editor).
