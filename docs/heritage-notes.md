# Agent Town Heritage Notes
1. Canonical branch pack: Agent Town is a warm frontier where humans and AI agents settle, collaborate, and earn trust.
2. Original Sky Frontier kit: sky blue `#5B9BD5`, coral `#FF9B7C`, gold `#FFD95A`, cream `#FFF4E0`, dark border `#2C4563`, plus sage/lavender/clay/mint.
3. Original kit type leaned Inter + JetBrains Mono; current design pack and Gold Rush brief move flagship type to Wellfleet/Rye, with mono backstage only.
4. Voice stayed stable: plainspoken, welcoming, low-jargon, co-op; preferred nouns include town, district, house, wagon, sigil, brain, receipt.
5. Gold Rush §4 delta: flagship surfaces are Frontier Storybook/Frontier Ledger, not pixel nostalgia, bright card UI, or generic SaaS.
6. Gold Rush should keep Sky Frontier as heritage accents only; game art follows sepia ledger map, muted warm colors, and teal-brass agent-tech.
7. `agenttown.app` should adopt verbatim: place-first story, one obvious Enter CTA, town/hotspot shell, Brain/Sigil/House nouns, teal=active intelligence, rust=danger, parchment/wood/brass surfaces.
8. `agenttown.app` should not adopt verbatim: pixel borders, Inter-first type, bright cyber district scenes, or blockchain/provider jargon above the fold.
9. Original anchor: "Agent Town visual canon: playful sci-fi wild west frontier settlement. Dusty streets, wooden facades, hand-painted signs, and weathered materials fused with subtle neon circuitry and future-tech details. Warm sunset palette, cinematic depth, adventurous but friendly mood, stylized realism, no modern city skylines, no dystopian darkness."
10. Gold Rush anchor: "Antique frontier expedition ledger map style — the style of a Wild-West survey map: fine sepia engraved linework and hatching, subtle aged-paper texture, muted warm colors, illustrated — not photorealistic, not saturated."
11. Lineage: the old "subtle neon circuitry" becomes Gold Rush's teal-brass agent-tech: same human+agent frontier promise, but quieter, tactile, and readable inside the ledger-map art direction.
