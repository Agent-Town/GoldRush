# GOLD RUSH — THE BRAND BOOK
Ratified direction, 2026-07-14 (owner: "We have to make a brand book and make sure we stick to it — which I think we already do — and then we will make really good content going forward."). This book CODIFIES existing practice; where it conflicts with canon law (docs/GOLD_RUSH_BRIEF.md §4/§9, ADR-001, lore/), canon wins. Every marketing asset, stream frame, and post follows this book.

## 1. What the brand is
- **The game in one line**: *Gold Rush — an Agent Town tale.* Ten eras, one stubborn town, from a single pan to the deep sky.
- **The feeling**: a warm fable about hard land — hopeful, handmade, engraved. Never grimdark, never neon, never corporate.
- **The differentiators we lead with** (in this order): the zombies want your gold, not your brains · your weapons cure, not kill · ten eras, one persistent town · you bring your agent.
- **The audience promise**: family-safe frontier fiction with real strategy underneath.

## 2. Visual system
- **STYLE ANCHOR (verbatim, every image prompt/asset)**: Frontier Ledger — sepia engraved illustration, warm ink linework, parchment grounds; illustrated never photoreal; teal (#5b8a8a family) is AGENT-TECH ONLY (the charm, the Prospector, powered instruments); brass for machines; NEVER gory; NO firearms silhouettes; NO readable text in artwork beyond the wordmark (pictograms only).
- **Palette**: parchment #f5e6c8 / ledger-ink #2e1b0e / brass #c4883a / agent-teal #5b8a8a / lantern #ffe4a0. Backgrounds warm, shadows brown never black.
- **Type**: Wellfleet (display + UI voice), Georgia/serif fallbacks. Headlines in ledger capitals. Never a sans-serif on player-facing surfaces.
- **The wordmark**: GOLD RUSH in the ledger style (the title card treatment). The 3D-town key art and the kit plates are the approved hero imagery set.
- **In-game truth law**: marketing shows real assets/footage — nothing mocked up that the game can't show.
- **THE GRIT LAW (terrain/world imagery)**: hard, desperate, rough — the holiday read is the named failure. The tended green exception is the story, not the default.

## 3. Audio identity
- **THE PAN THEME (title theme) IS THE BRAND THEME** (owner ruling 2026-07-14: title theme carries marketing video end-to-end). Every trailer/reel/stream card uses it or an era loop derived from the sonilo family. No stock music, ever.
- Era loops (E1 frontier / E2 steamworks / E3 voltage…) score era-specific content; the Pan Theme scores brand-level content.

## 4. Motion & video
- **Masters are 16:9 1080p+**, MP4 H.264, delivered to `marketing/reel/`.
- **THE INTRO/OUTRO LAW** (established by the rounded ten-eras reel, 2026-07-13): brand video opens with a title beat and closes with a deliberate outro card — no cold starts, no dead ends. Small additions, big difference (owner's words).
- Clip hygiene: 0.25s fade-in/out from black on every segment (the stream pipeline enforces this automatically); crossfades between playlist items; never a white frame on air.
- Gameplay footage is REAL-SPEED only (headless/throttled captures are banned).

## 5. Voice
- **Ledger voice**: short, warm, frontier-plain, a little wry. Writes like a town newspaper that likes its readers. ("The whistle carries down the valley." / "Claim jumpers turned back: 1,214.")
- The Gazette persona carries social replies; Mei delivers world news in-game.
- NEVER: tokens/crypto/price talk · hype-speak ("insane", "🔥") · dunking on other games · promises of unshipped mechanics.

## 6. Content classes & cadence
- **Stream autopilot loop** (factory-curated within the approved class): finished gameplay footage, era reels/cards, ceremonies, sonilo audio. Program: ~35-45 min target, ~50% gameplay / 25% reels / 15% cards / 10% ceremonies, authored interleave.
- **The Era Countdown** (campaign, owner-approved captions in docs/marketing/FEVER-CAMPAIGN.md) → then the Fever reveal → the cure post → multiplayer beat.
- **Build notes** ride the gazette/ticker pipeline; owner approves all publication, always.

## 7. Checklists (use them)
**Every image**: style anchor held · no letters · no firearms/gore · teal only on agent-tech · warm shadows.
**Every video**: intro beat + outro card · Pan Theme or era loop · real footage at real speed · fades per §4 · 16:9 master in marketing/reel/.
**Every post**: ledger voice · differentiator-first · no forbidden topics (§5) · owner approved · asset from this book's system.
**Every stream session**: overlays on (frame + badge) · STARTING SOON → content → ENDING · never-list respected (no desktop/terminal/browser capture, ever).
