## §1 POSITIONING

**One paragraph.** Gold Rush is a cozy survivors–tower-defense roguelite set in the Agent Town universe: you stake a river claim with a brass pan, hold it through the night, and grow a town through ten eras of invention — steam, current, motors, the sea, the atom, signal, orbit, a red world, the deep sky — until the children are handed the machine that builds worlds. Nothing loved is erased: the town transforms forward, the old maps stay playable forever, and the same pan is at the hero's hip in era one and era ten. It is drawn as one hand-engraved, parchment-warm world across hundreds of images, and it is built in the open by one family and a factory of AI agents — a game about machine citizens, made with them. The gold rush was never about the gold.

**One line.** *One claim, ten eras, nothing loved is erased — a hand-drawn frontier that grows from a brass pan to the stars.*

**The three hooks + the pillar under them:**
- **H1 — The factory:** a family-built game with AI co-founders, shipping real slices in the open (the making-of IS content; see VISION-HOOKS' live-factory idea).
- **H2 — The saga (LEAD):** ten eras, one town, a century of progress that never resets — every era re-answers "what is a claim?"
- **H3 — The handover:** survivors×TD×roguelite that ends by handing kids a world-builder (the Charter Press).
- **PILLAR (cross-cutting, per owner 2026-07-12):** *the consistent universe* — one coherent illustrated world, AI-generated and proud of it, the same engraved hand across ten eras. The pillar is how every hook is SHOWN.

**Why H2 leads (3 sentences).** The reset-ache is the one emotion every survivors/roguelite player already owns — the owner himself named it ("it is a bit sad that the map and the buildings reset after each game"), and "your town never resets across a century" answers it in six words. It is the only hook we can prove TODAY with post-ready assets — the era chains and the ten-eras reel literally show one hill keeping everything as it transforms, so the lead hook and the visual pillar are the same picture. And the other two hooks live comfortably inside its telling: the family+AI factory is HOW a saga this size exists, and the kids' world-builder is where it ends — a mystery the saga is allowed to keep.
