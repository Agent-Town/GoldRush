## 0. The finding in one paragraph

A human has **one** freely-positioned body: the hero. The Prospector follows it by drift and can be sent to exactly one class of destination — a published seam or sluice — by the dispatch control. A rider has **two** freely-positioned bodies: the hero through `MOVE_HERO`, and the Prospector through `MOVE_TO`, `HOLD` and `FALLBACK_IF`. That second body is not a cosmetic difference, because in the engine the benchmark scores (`HeadlessContractSim`) **every reach test on the agent side reads the Prospector's position** — capture, fund, recover, plant, redig, backfill, stoke, grade, haul, the interference-front mute, the build placement radius, the repair radius. In the browser those same tests read `this.actionActor.group.position`, the hero. So the rider does not merely have an extra movement verb; it has an extra *arm*, and the whole context-action family hangs off it.

The 1:1 grammar therefore is not "delete `MOVE_TO`". It is **retire the three Prospector-positioning verbs and re-base GR-SIM's reach tests onto the hero**, which is where the human's are already. Deleting the verbs alone would leave a rider unable to reach a canal stake, a crater, a vent or a corridor at all.

## 3. The 1:1 grammar proposal

### 3a. The shape

**Retire three verbs, re-base one, re-base the reach tests, and give both species the same one new policy.**

| # | change | why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Remove `MOVE_TO`, `HOLD`, `FALLBACK_IF`.** | All three position the Prospector. ADR-005 forbids that outright and forbids giving the human the same power, so there is no "both sides gain it" cure available: the only 1:1 is removal. |
| 2 | **Re-base GR-SIM's ACTION reach tests from `this.prospector.position` to `this.hero.group.position`**, matching the browser's `this.actionActor.group.position`. The nine sites: `capture` (`src/sim/HeadlessContractSim.ts:1404`), `contextAction`'s upgrade/demolish (`:2967-2968`), `stokeVent` (`:2942`), `recoverProbe` (`:2986`), `plantSeedVault` (`:3006`), `decideCanalSegment` (`:3028`), `motorVerb` grade and haul (`:3055-3056`), `usePlaybook`'s interference test (`:3093`), `fundMegaproject` (`:3328-3329`). The engine already knows how to make this choice per contract: `:619` and `:1309` pick between the two bodies on the deepwater and race-course twists. | Without this, removing `MOVE_TO` makes the whole context-action family unreachable in the scoring engine. With it, the rider walks the hero to the stake exactly as the human does, and `MOVE_HERO` is the verb that gets it there. |
| 2b | **Do NOT re-base three Prospector reads.** The pan reach (`:2831`, `Balance.goldSeam.channelRange` from the Prospector) is correct as-is: the human's dispatch pans at the Prospector too. The repair reach (`:2822`) stays for the same reason. The E8 suit/crossing reads (`:2038`, `:2042`, `:2045`) belong to the air question and to the `e8-air-logical` slice launched the same morning, not here. | Parity means the same body doing the same job, not one body doing every job. Harvest and repair are Prospector chores on both sides today; that is already 1:1. |
| 3 | **Bound `REPAIR_UNDER` to the human's radius.** Today its target search is `state.buildings.find(...)` with no distance term at all (`src/agent/StandingOrders.ts:501-506`) and its travel clause carries the Prospector to whatever it finds anywhere on the map. The human's sweep only considers works within `Balance.sparkRig.range` (10 wu) of the Prospector, which drifts to the hero (`src/game/Game.ts:8115-8127`). Add the same radius, measured from the Prospector, and let drift do the rest. | The policy NAME is already 1:1 ("repair under N%"). The REACH is not. This is the second unfair advantage after `MOVE_TO`, and it is bigger in traffic: 2,561 orders against `MOVE_TO`'s 372. |
| 4 | **`BOAT_BUILD` and `REANCHOR`: give the HUMAN the control, do not remove the verbs.** These reach a real E5 mechanic whose only browser lever is `?debug`. Under ADR-005 clause 2 the human gains it: a deck-pad prompt on the confirm key while the hero stands on the boat, and an anchor list in the same context prompt. | Removing them would delete an epoch's mechanic from the door; the ruling's own remedy for a thin human surface is to thicken it. This is Mistake #10 in its original form: a mechanic the player cannot see in a plain boot. |
| 5 | **Publish the four confirm-key interactions or record them as human-only.** The drill yard, the assay bench, the E10 static boss and the Old Digger have no verb and no view field. | `human-only-richer` is not a violation of ADR-005 (no unfair advantage to the agent), so this is a decision, not a duty. Recording it is the duty. |
| 6 | **Add ONE control both species gain: a `WORK_AT` / "send to work" policy over published work targets.** The human already has half of it (dispatch to a seam or sluice). Widen it, on both sides, to any target the view already publishes as work: seams, sluices, damaged works, the megaproject site, a corridor stake, a canal stake, a vent, a crater. The rider's verb takes a published target ID; the human's dispatch accepts a click on the same targets. | This is the honest answer to "the human's surface is thinner than a fair game needs". It restores the *useful* half of `MOVE_TO` (get the Prospector to the thing) without restoring the *unfair* half (get it to an arbitrary point), and it gives it to both species in the same act. **Owner decision required** — it is new player-facing scope, so it is proposed, not assumed. |

`SET_WEAPON`, `PICK_UPGRADE`, `SECURE_CHOICE`, `BLAST_AT`, `BUILD`, `HARVEST`, `MOVE_HERO`, `GRADE`, `HAUL`, `CAPTURE`, `PLAYBOOK_USE` and `CONTEXT_ACTION` keep their grammar unchanged. Only their acting body moves (change 2).

### 3b. What a rider's plan looks like afterwards

Before (E4 Boneyard, real shape from the heat-12 tapes):

```
[{"verb":"MOVE_TO","pos":{"x":12,"z":-4}},{"verb":"GRADE"},{"verb":"HAUL"},{"verb":"HOLD","pos":{"x":0,"z":8}}]
```

After:

```
[{"verb":"MOVE_HERO","pos":{"x":12,"z":-4}},{"verb":"GRADE"},{"verb":"HAUL"}]
```

The plan is shorter, the arithmetic is identical, and it is the same sequence of acts a human performs: walk there, press confirm. The trailing `HOLD` disappears because the hero has no drift to hold against — the reason there is no `HOLD_HERO` today (`src/agent/StandingOrders.ts:34-39`).

### 3c. Consequences for existing tapes (ADR-004 retirement)

Measured, not estimated:

- **Tapes carrying at least one removed verb: 57 of 89. Scored tapes: 22 of 24, across 22 of the 24 scored rides.** (`HOLD` alone accounts for 57 tapes / 22 scored; `MOVE_TO` for 24 / 10; `FALLBACK_IF` for 0.) **Only two scored heat-12 tapes survive the grammar change.**
- Those tapes stop *validating*, not merely stop reproducing: `validateOrder` returns `orders[i].verb "HOLD" is unknown` (`src/agent/StandingOrders.ts:949`), so replay fails at submission rather than diverging mid-run. That is the clean failure ADR-004 retirement wants.
- `REPAIR_UNDER`'s radius (change 3) is a *semantic* change, not a grammar one: its tapes still validate and will diverge. **14 tapes / 5 scored** carry it. Their hashes move.
- Change 2 (the body re-base) moves the hash of every tape carrying a re-based verb: **37 tapes / 15 scored** carry at least one of `CONTEXT_ACTION`, `CAPTURE`, `GRADE`, `HAUL`, `PLAYBOOK_USE`, `BOAT_BUILD`, `REANCHOR`.
- **Net: the heat-12 board does not survive this change.** 22 of 24 scored rows retire under ADR-004 clause 3, and the surviving two are re-scored on a re-based engine. The board is re-ridden, not repaired. Counts by `artifacts/rider-parity-audit/count-heat12-verbs.mjs` plus a union pass over the same 89 tapes.

### 3d. Consequences for the provers that secure today

Every prover under `artifacts/*/prover.mjs` that plans with a positioning verb has to be re-ridden. The ones this audit can name from the ride data are the E4 Motor family (all four maps used `MOVE_TO` to reach corridor stakes), the E8 Orbital family (all four used `MOVE_TO` or `HOLD` to reach dome air), `e1-baron`, `e2-hill-mine`, `e7-relay-valley`, `e7-dead-band`, `e9-dome-basin` and `the-claim`.

The two that need a *policy* rewrite rather than a re-ride:

1. **E8 Orbital.** `HOLD` on a dome pad is how a rider breathes today. After the change the rider walks the *hero* to the dome and the Prospector drifts in behind it — which is exactly what a human does, and what `docs/decisions/ADR-005` is for. But it changes the air arithmetic: the drift target is `(hero.x - 1.8, hero.z - 1.25)` (`src/agent/Embodiment.ts:288-291`), so the Prospector sits ~2.2 wu behind the hero and a dome pad smaller than that radius will not hold both. **This needs measuring against every dome radius before the grammar lands**, and it interacts directly with the `e8-air-logical` slice launched the same morning.
2. **E4 Motor.** `GRADE`/`HAUL` read the Prospector's ground; after change 2 they read the hero's. The provers stage the Hauler at corridor stakes, so the whole staging sequence is re-derived. Cheap to re-ride, but every motor tape's hash moves.

`e10-ember-shore`'s prover (`artifacts/e10s-4-door/prover.mjs`) uses `stoke`, which is a Prospector-ground action today; after change 2 the hero stands in the vent disc instead, which is the browser's own rule already (`src/game/Game.ts:9495`), so that prover converges *toward* the browser rather than away.

### 3e. Order of operations

The grammar change cannot land in one slice without breaking the board mid-flight. The draft master `tasks/rider-parity-grammar.md` sequences it:

1. Re-base the reach tests (change 2) **first**, as a no-grammar-change slice. Every tape still validates; hashes move once, measurably, with the old verbs still available to reach the stakes.
2. Bound `REPAIR_UNDER` (change 3).
3. Remove `MOVE_TO`, `HOLD`, `FALLBACK_IF` (change 1) and retire the tapes in the same commit.
4. `BOAT_BUILD`/`REANCHOR` human controls (change 4) as its own E5 slice.
5. `WORK_AT` (change 6) only on an owner word.
