## The three results (all: 100% of ARC-AGI-3's 25 public games, superhuman action efficiency)
1. **Tycho** (arxiv 2607.28287): a coding-agent that constructs game-specific EXECUTABLE world models during interaction (model/test/plan/repair/bypass), the core contribution being "active abstraction" — deciding when acquiring or using a model is worth its cost. Opus 5 / GPT-5.6: 100 RHAE, all 183 levels, 61% fewer scored actions than human baselines.
2. **VISTA** (MIT, vista-research.github.io): the minimalist opposite — raw PNG frames, lossless memory recall, long-horizon vision on a general multimodal model (Opus 5), no task-specific engineering. 100% win, 56% fewer actions than first-time humans.
3. **AVO** (arxiv 2603.24517): agents as evolutionary VARIATION OPERATORS in autonomous multi-day search (demonstrated on GPU kernels, beating cuDNN/FlashAttention-4). The general amplifier: what long-running agentic search now achieves unattended.
