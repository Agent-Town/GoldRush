### F-ER01-E9-1 — Dome Basin's canal and epoch-wide arsenal are socketed; Old Digger and two arsenal consumers still stop at the browser boundary

`E9CanalSocket` drives the production `E9CanalSystem` with per-instance null-backed tile storage. Two independent 210-tick drives produce the same `sha256:d2cb3ebbb6df6650e02218a3787e9bd53cd0b93dec7da1e92310265c844949cf` snapshot after reaching C3, wetting the canal, landing `e9-canal-quarry-payout-ice-pack-1`, and recording 31 dust-devil shoves / 6.2 pushed distance. Its local refusal count remains honest: 210 `E9ArsenalSystem` steps and 210 `OldDiggerBossSystem` steps are outside that canal-only drive.

`E9ArsenalSocket` now drives the production `E9ArsenalSystem.update` seam for every Red Fields contract. Two independent 181-tick Dome Basin drives produce the same `sha256:e0a342a149872fa4680be98c989780c7cea6ddf952307544d9375bbb1363f203` snapshot after reaching the 30-charge cap, deploying one Storm Fence, pushing an enemy 0.08, and recording one denial tick. Each drive also counts 181 refused `CombatSystem.update` ticks and 181 refused `EnemyPool.update` ticks: the former means registered shooters never fire and `fires` / Cure-Arms `outcomes` stay empty; the latter means the measured fence slow is integrated by nothing. `OldDiggerBossSystem:627` still queries the canvas. `HeadlessContractSim` still rejects the contract, the derived manifest still reports only `build_zones`, and the contract still declares no `engineDependencies`; the attended fix must run those two arsenal consumers, socket Old Digger, derive the action/view/event vocabulary, and add honest dependency staging before admission.
