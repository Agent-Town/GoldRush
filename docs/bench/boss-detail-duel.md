# THE BOSS DETAIL DUEL — Opus 5 (max) vs GPT-5.6-Sol (xhigh)
Commissioned 2026-07-27 (owner: "the bosses are low poly right now, could be higher polygon count, more detail... That might be a good benchmark"). IDENTICAL brief, identical bosses, disjoint outputs, pre-registered rubric.

## The contest
Both contestants upgrade THE SAME TWO BOSSES — **the Dredge Queen** (the flagship: richest plate + choreography canon) and **the Salvage Claw** — from their shipped low-poly models to high-detail versions. Budget ceiling raised for the duel: ≤45k tris/boss, ≤2×2048 atlases, engraved-house materials (never photoreal), byte-identical re-export, canon §9/ADR-001 absolute.
Outputs (disjoint by filename): `<boss>-detail-opus5.glb` / `<boss>-detail-sol.glb` + turntables + tri/material stats + a self-report.

## The rubric (pre-registered; judged blind where possible)
- 40% FIDELITY: to the boss's plate + storybook choreography (the Queen's claws that cycle, the Claw's crown/winch/feet acts) + the era palette.
- 20% CRAFT LEGALITY: budgets, re-export determinism, mount/anchor compatibility (drop-in for the shipped system).
- 20% PERFORMANCE: measured draw cost at gameplay camera (the duel includes loading both into the boot rig and reading renderer.info).
- 20% THE OWNER'S EYE: Robin picks blind A/B turntables per boss.
Winner's models ship (per boss — split verdicts allowed); loser's work archived per retention law. Both self-reports feed the factory's model-choice doctrine.
