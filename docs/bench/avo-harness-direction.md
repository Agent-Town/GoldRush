## The three lessons, compressed
- **Tycho**: turn interaction into an executable world model; plan inside it where rollouts are free. Its "active abstraction" = choosing which state variables matter.
- **VISTA**: near-zero scaffolding beat structured pipelines — frontier models carry game priors, and a harness can SUBTRACT capability with the wrong representation.
- **AVO**: the model need not be right, only generative — a population of candidate policies, the LLM as a semantically-informed mutation operator, the environment as selection.

## Why Gold Rush is the AVO dream condition
ARC harnesses had to LEARN their world model. Ours is GIVEN: the sim is open, deterministic per seed, seconds per rollout — and sim-import is RULED lawful with disclosure (owner, 2026-08-24). The genome already exists in county law: **playbooks** ("the genome is the graph, GR-SIM is the fitness function" — ap-15's own sentence). Quality-diversity selection maps onto the ap-15 axes; the Surveyor's and Homesteader's crowns are a multi-objective fitness function we already designed.

## The honest prediction, and why the experiment still matters
Such a harness likely clears EVERY admitted map — because the admission program guarantees a public-verb solution exists (proved ×2 per door). So the experiment is a CALIBRATION INSTRUMENT, not a moonshot. Its three separable yields:
1. Any map even search struggles with → flagged for a tuning/legibility audit (that residue would be OUR defect class).
2. The one-shot-vs-search gap, measured per map → the benchmark's most interesting number (comprehension vs search).
3. The evolved tape library → the baseline the efficiency ladders rank against.

## The difficulty decomposition (is "harder than ARC" our mistake?)
1. **Genuine depth** (not a mistake): long horizons + economy + placement + compounding errors; heat-1's baron rides reached w21 twice unsecured — survival without victory-comprehension.
2. **Interface friction** (our debt, fixable): the heats' door findings (transport framing, verdict selectors); the open VISTA-flavored question — does our JSON view DEACTIVATE priors a screenshot would activate? A visual-door experiment would answer it.
3. **Knife-edge tuning** (a design stance, owner's to revisit): admission ladders ship one rung inside the securing boundary — expert-calibrated, no slack for explorers. ARC leaves margin; we often do not.
