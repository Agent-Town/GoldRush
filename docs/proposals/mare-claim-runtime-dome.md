# Mare Claim runtime dome

The playable central air pad has no dome. The factory preview imports the Town Dome Commons plate, then excludes that helper from the terrain export. Native desktop/mobile panning and refill pass, but the breathing area is visually empty.

A town-plate shell transplant does not meet the map need. Evidence is in `artifacts/map-art-repairs-20260908/mare-dome-candidate-01` through `03`: the first is a teal cage; removing its panel grid leaves thin ribs. At scale 0.4, its 21.65-unit radius becomes 8.66 while the central air rectangle remains x/z ±6. Its doorway is about 1.41 units high and faces away from the initial approach. The original asset remains unchanged.

The repair should use the landmark factory's existing 3,000-triangle budget, one material and 1024px atlas. Reuse the existing brass/silver material vocabulary and concept plate, but author a map body with a visibly grounded 12-by-12 base corresponding to the air pad, a domed upper silhouette, and a south-facing opening at least 2.4 units tall and 2.2 wide. Keep the pad interior traversable and the player readable under the roof. The doorway threshold must not introduce a step or obstacle that contradicts the existing movement mask. Show the atmosphere boundary through the architecture rather than an invisible larger or smaller area.

Verify initial approach and interior views on desktop and 390px mobile, compare with the concept, and repeat the native paid-pan/refill route. If the body is reused for the other two pads, each mount needs its own Object3D instance; the loader now loads an independent scene and material set for each placement; the repeat-placement browser check passes on desktop and mobile. Do not move the Far Side or Eclipse shared dressing without applying their existing alias rulings.
