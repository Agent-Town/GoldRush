# The Ten-Epoch Saga — from the river claim to the stars
### Master content plan · DRAFT v1 for owner review · 2026-07-07

Owner directive (verbatim): "Make a plan for the next ten epochs, from frontier to scifi in the stars. All clear, logical steps deducted from humanities development. Make plans for the contracts, the houses, the tech, the science, the upgrades and inventions, new weapons, new ideas. Where will things head and how do they connect… define art, crafts, everything… And then the final milestone is to use all of this experience to make a new contract builder that even my kids can use."

**How to read this:** every epoch below fills the SAME bundle shape the engine already consumes (Epoch-Contract Law: contract vocabulary, card families, mode verbs, tech-language, art manifest, tile descriptor). Epochs 2–4 are specified near-build depth; 5–10 are specified to full field coverage but deliberately looser — play will teach us, and each epoch gets its complete bundle-spec at build time. Inherited laws bind everything: illustrated never gory · frontier-tech, no realistic firearms ever (ADR-001 trajectory: brass → agent-tech) · satire playful, never textbook · prosperity framing · enemies are outlaws, rival companies, machines, and nature — never peoples (§9.3) · the town transforms forward, tiles are era-stamped and replayable forever · generator proposes, contract disposes, at every scale.

## 2. Cross-epoch systems (the connective tissue)

**Economy line.** Gold never dies (satire: whatever the era, somebody wants gold) — but each epoch adds its era-resource that powers the new systems: pressure, current, crude, salvage, isotopes, signal, helium-3, water, starlight. LAW: one new primary resource per epoch, max two active alongside gold, older ones auto-convert at the Claim Office ("the market moved on"). Prevents resource sprawl; keeps each era's economy legible.

**Weapon lineage (frontier-tech law).** One inventive contraption family per era, никогда firearms — each is a plausible child of the last: Spark Rig & Blast Charge (E1) → **Boiler Lance** (steam jet) & **Pressure Mortar** (E2) → **Arc Caster** & **Coil Fence** (electrified palisade tier) (E3) → **Piston Slinger** & **Gusher Tar-Sprayer** (slows, never burns people — warm law) (E4) → **Harpoon Ballista** & **Depth Charge Lobber** (E5) → **Sunline Beam** & **Half-Life Caltrops** (glow, not gore) (E6) → **Signal Jammer** (turns machine enemies) & **Drone Companions** (E7) → **Magnet Grapple** & **Solar Lens Array** (E8) → **Terraform Cannon** (moves earth AT enemies — playful) & **Storm Fence** (E9) → **The Starlight Pan** (E10: the humble pan returns as the endgame weapon — panning constellations into bolts; full circle). Every era: turret + wall + lob archetypes persist (BT trio law), reskinned and re-teched.

**Enemy faction lineage (canon-safe forever).** Claim jumpers & bandits (E1) → rail-baron toughs & steam wreckers (E2) → Current-War saboteurs & wire thieves (E3, Tesla/Edison satire as rival companies) → motor gangs & gusher blowouts (E4) → corsair skiffs, machine-leviathans, storms (E5) → the Combine's feral appliances (E6 — overclocked toasters gone bad; warm robot comedy) → rogue automata & data-rustlers (E7) → scrap corsairs & debris storms (E8) → dust devils & feral terraformers (E9) → **the Static** (an entropy storm-faction; the void itself as the final rival company) (E10). Three enemy classes per epoch: a RUSHER, a SIEGER (attacks buildings), a THIEF (steals the era-resource) — the E1 triangle, re-expressed forever.

**The Prospector's evolution (and the agents' story).** E1: brass hover-automaton, one helper. E2: gains steam-tools. E3: gains a charging dock (power grid tie-in). E5: waterproofing (the first "outfit" moment). E6: THE LORE TURN — the Calculating House is built and the town realizes helpers can be *made*: recruited agents (M6) become era-canon here retroactively told. E7: playbooks — agents learn jobs by WATCHING the player do them once (the brief's promise, shipped as a mechanic: record → name → delegate). E8+: an agent CREW with roles; E10: the Ark's intelligence — all your agents, generations of them, are the ship. The permission-ladder/consent law never bends: autonomy is always granted, never assumed, at every scale.

**Town & house lineages (the tavern law: the tavern is the heart, always).** Core buildings transform, never disappear:
- **Tavern** → Saloon → Electric Lounge → Motor Inn → Harbor House → Atomic Diner → Net Café → Orbital Canteen → Dome Commons → **the Long Table** (the Ark's mess — ten eras of stories on one wall). The contract board lives here in every era.
- **Schoolhouse** → Academy → Polytechnic → … → the Bridge School (Ark) — science's home; children learn here, then take the era's new roles.
- **Assay Office** → Fabrication Works → Pattern Bureau → … → **the Charter Press** (E10 — crafting's endpoint is world-crafting; §4 below).
- **Claim Office** → Land Bureau → Charter Authority (contracts/tiles/market); **Chapel** → Meeting Hall → Observatory (the quiet building looks upward era by era — warm, non-denominational); **Store, Bank, Sheriff's Watch** evolve alongside.
Time passes between runs (weddings, births, schooling — tavern-ledger vignettes first, sim later); epoch transitions age the cast one generation. Portraits get AGING VARIANTS (image-edit transforms per the asset strategy — consistency, not regeneration).

**Science branches.** Always three, era-named skins of the same trio: economy / arsenal / fabrication-agent (Prospecting→Geology→Grid Craft→Petroleum Arts→Tide Craft→Isotope Science→Signal Theory→Astrogation→Areology→Cosmography · Arsenal keeps its name, tools change · Assay→Fabrication→…→Charter Craft). Node counts grow 15→24 across the arc; thresholds per the table; overflow always banks forward; Continued Study always exists at the ceiling.

**Mode-manifest verbs (the builder's growing palette).** defend, pan, build (E1) → escort (rail runs) (E2) → connect/power (E3) → drive, race, convoy (E4) → dive, salvage, weather (E5) → decay-timer, hazard-zone (E6) → record, program, delegate (E7) → launch, orbit, dock (E8) → terraform, persist (E9) → compose, share (E10). Every verb ships engine-side with its epoch and becomes a toy in the kids' builder the moment it exists.

## 3. The epochs in detail

### Epoch 2 — THE STEAMWORKS (next up; build-depth spec)
**Story beat.** The rail spur arrives; the claim becomes a company town. Pressure is progress — and the rail barons want the route.
**Extraction.** Gold continues (stamp mill multiplies sluice yield); NEW: **Pressure** — boilers generate it, machines consume it; over-pressure is both a resource bank and a hazard (vent or boom — warm boom: comedic steam clouds, never harm to people).
**Tile.** *The Hill Mine* — the FIRST elevation tile (GT ladder's debut): a terraced hillside with a mine mouth, switchback paths (slope movement matters), a rail cut below. High ground for turrets; a ravine funnels waves.
**Buildings.** NEW: Boiler House (pressure hub), Stamp Mill (megaproject residue → economy building), Rail Depot (escort contracts start here), Machine Shop (tier-4 unlocks). TRANSFORMS: sluice → steam sluice (wheel becomes piston), palisade → iron-banded wall, turret → boiler-fed battery (fire rate scales with pressure!).
**Science (threshold 8, ~18 nodes).** Geology (vein-mapping: reveal ore seams on the tile map, +stamp yield) · Arsenal (pressure weapons, coil groundwork) · Fabrication (contract v2: steam parts, +2 order slots, first recruited-agent SLOT unlock — one helper hired at the Depot).
**Contract family v2.** Materials: iron, brass, steam-seals, gauge-glass. Verbs: pressurize, gear, vent. Stat domains gain `pressureCost`/`pressureBurst`. Example approved order: *"a pressure-fed pan that shakes itself"* → Auto-Pan (tool, uncommon, panTick −25%, pressure upkeep 2).
**Cards.** New families: Pressure (venting bursts as AoE), Rail (escort-run bonuses), Iron (wall/repair). Mastery: Pressure×Arsenal → "Overdrive Whistle" (all turrets +40% for 10s, then vent).
**Weapons.** Boiler Lance (steam-jet cone), Pressure Mortar (lob, replaces nothing — sits beside Blast Charge). Defense: iron wall tier, boiler-fed battery.
**Enemies.** Rail-baron toughs (rusher, shielded vs bolts — steam beats them), steam wreckers (sieger: giant wrench-crabs), coal thieves. Boss-wave: the Baron's armored railcar (rides the rail cut — the level's own geography attacks).
**Town life.** First WEDDING event (two townsfolk from E1's portraits); the schoolhouse's first graduate becomes the Depot clerk. Tavern gains the contract BOARD physically (Town v1).
**Mode verbs.** escort (protect the ore car along the rail), pressurize (hold N boilers hot through a wave).
**Art manifest (~batch scale).** Hill-mine terrain set, 4 new buildings + 5 transforms, 3 enemies + railcar boss, 6 townsfolk additions + 8 aging variants, weapon/icon set. (~60–80 generations incl. retries — nightly drip, two weeks ahead of need.)
**Engine tech needed.** GT-02/03 (slopes+routing) ✓in flight · multi-tile travel via tavern board (Town v1) · megaproject build-site mechanic (multi-run construction with defense phases) · rail entity (path-follower) · pressure network-lite (per-building resource, no full graph yet) · **AUDIO MILESTONE** (the world gains sound here: steam, rails, tavern piano — the game has been silent; flag for owner: budget/source).

### Epoch 3 — THE VOLTAGE AGE (build-depth spec)
**Story beat.** Two rival power companies electrify the frontier and sabotage each other; the town picks no side and wires itself. Night stops being downtime.
**Extraction.** Copper veins; **Current** (watts) generated at the Dynamo Hall, ROUTED along wires you string — the first true network mechanic.
**Tile.** *The Canyon Works* — vertical drama (GT mature): a dam site, cliff galleries, wire spans crossing the gorge. NIGHT WAVES: half of each run in the dark; light = safety = powered.
**Buildings.** NEW: Dynamo Hall (megaproject → generator), pylons/wire posts (buildable network), Arc Lamps (area light + slow-aura), Tram Station (in-tile transport). TRANSFORMS: batteries → arc turrets (chain lightning between POWERED turrets — placement puzzle), tavern → Electric Lounge (neon signage).
**Science (10, ~20 nodes).** Grid Craft (transmission loss ↓, battery storage) · Arsenal (arc weapons, coil fence) · Fabrication v3 (electric components; agent charging dock = Prospector uptime ↑; 2nd recruited agent).
**Contract v3.** Materials: copper coil, filament, insulator. Verbs: charge, arc, illuminate. New stat domain: `wattDraw`. Example: *"a lamp that scares thieves"* → Watchman's Filament (utility: thieves flee lit areas 2s).
**Cards.** Grid family (powered-structure buffs), Night family (bonuses in darkness — risk/reward), Chain family. Mastery: Grid×Prospecting → "Electroplating" (powered sluices auto-refine +15%).
**Weapons/defense.** Arc Caster (chains), Coil Fence (wall tier that stuns), Arc Lamp (soft CC). The grid itself is the meta-weapon: cut wires = dark = danger.
**Enemies.** Current-War saboteurs (cut YOUR wires — defend the network, not just the base), wire thieves (steal copper), moth-swarms (nature: mob the lamps). Boss: the Rival Dynamo Crawler (drains your grid as it advances).
**New idea.** Brown-out decisions: not enough current for everything — a POWER LEDGER panel (what's lit, what's armed, what's dark tonight). Strategy layer with zero new resources to learn.
**Engine tech.** Power graph (nodes/edges/flow-lite) · day/night cycle + light-radius gameplay · wire rendering · tram path entity. Art: canyon set, 5 buildings + 6 transforms, lamps/pylons, 3 enemies + crawler, night-palette variants of EVERYTHING (paletted, cheap), 6 townsfolk + aging.

### Epoch 4 — THE MOTOR FRONTIER (build-depth spec)
**Story beat.** Oil under the dust flats; the frontier gets FAST. Distance becomes the resource and the threat — engines eat miles.
**Extraction.** Crude (derricks — nodding pumps, the new "sluice"), refined at the Refinery into Fuel (vehicles) and Tar (defense).
**Tile.** *The Dust Flats* — the biggest tile yet, deliberately too big to walk: you DRIVE. Roads you grade (cheap terraform-lite), derrick fields to claim outward, dust storms roll through (visibility weather).
**Buildings.** NEW: Derrick, Refinery (megaproject → building), Garage (vehicle bay), Road Grader post, Watchtower (see through dust). TRANSFORMS: stockpile → tank farm, walls → guardrails+tar moats (slow fields).
**Vehicles (the new idea).** The Flivver (fast, fragile scout), the Hauler (moves resources/turrets! — mobile base pieces), the Prospector gets a sidecar (agent rides along — co-op teaser for the co-op milestone). Convoy contracts: escort fuel to the rail head against motor gangs — Mad-Max-warm, Wacky-Races energy.
**Science (12).** Petroleum Arts (yield, refining ratios) · Arsenal (piston/tar) · Fabrication v4 (engine parts; recruited agent #3 = a DRIVER — the first agent with a vehicle playbook).
**Contract v4.** Materials: piston, gasket, rubber, tar. Verbs: motorize, haul, slick. Stat: `speed`, `capacity`. Example: *"make my hauler amphibious"* → REJECTED at tier 1 (no hull vocabulary yet — "the works eye the horizon; the sea calls for a different science") — the rejection TEASES epoch 5. Rejections as foreshadowing: house rule from here on.
**Enemies.** Motor gangs (circling bikes — moving waves!), gusher blowouts (nature hazard spawning tar sprites), pipeline rustlers. Boss: the Land-Yacht (a rolling fortress; kill its wheels — first component-target boss).
**Engine tech.** Vehicle movement (accel/turn physics-lite on the flat sim — GT slopes interact) · convoy/follow AI · weather system (dust = fog volumes + gameplay visibility) · component-boss framework. **THE BOAT megaproject closes the epoch: built in the tank farm, hauled overland set-piece, launched — the ocean unlocks.**

Science: Tide Craft / Arsenal (harpoon, depth charge) / Fabrication v5 (hull vocabulary — the E4 rejection pays off). Engine: water volumes, boat physics, dive state, storm system. Art: sea sets, hulls, 4 enemies, harbor townsfolk, UNDERWATER palette.

### Epoch 6 — THE ATOMIC HOMESTEAD (full fields, looser)
Fifties-futurism satire, rigorously WARM (owner tone-check requested — see §6): starstone isotopes glow friendly teal; hazards are half-life PUDDLES that decay on visible timers (the new idea: everything about this era ticks — buffs, hazards, enemies all decay), never sickness imagery. ### Epoch 7 — THE SIGNAL ERA (full fields, looser)
Computing dawn as frontier: relay towers on ridgelines, signal as extractable spectrum, data-rustlers tapping your lines. THE mechanic epoch: **playbooks** — press record, do a job once (a patrol, a repair route, a panning circuit), name it, hand it to an agent. The brief's oldest promise ("tasks learned from how the player performs them") ships here as the era's core toy — and it is EXACTLY the kids' builder's little sibling: recording IS programming. Science: Signal Theory / Arsenal (jammer — turns machine enemies briefly) / Fabrication v7 (drone companions). Buildings: Relay Tower, Exchange (the net café tavern annex), Playbook Library (schoolhouse transform — lessons become literal programs). Enemies: rogue automata (your own playbooks, corrupted — teachable moment: debug them by watching the replay!), data-rustlers. Megaproject: **THE STARSHIP** (canonical), built across many runs, every prior era contributing a component contract (steam valves, copper coils, hull plate, reactor core, guidance playbooks — a deliberate all-history victory lap). Engine: record/replay of player action sequences (deterministic event-log foundation pays its dividend), playbook execution through the typed tool surface (all built — this is why the invariants were laws).

### Epoch 8 — THE ORBITAL FRONTIER (full fields, looser)

Solar storms as weather. Enemies: scrap corsairs (rival salvage companies), debris rain. Buildings: dome habitats (walls become AIR walls), launch pad, solar lens array. Science: Astrogation / Arsenal (magnet grapple — yank enemies into each other, warm and funny) / Fabrication v8 (vacuum vocabulary). Contracts: satellite claims (defend a beacon in orbit-tile), first EVA escort. Megaproject: the Colony Seed. Engine: gravity/atmosphere params per tile, breach mechanics. Art: the game goes silver-and-teal; Earth hangs in the sky over the parchment moon (the signature image of the back half).

### Epoch 9 — THE RED FIELDS (full fields, looser)
Planetary homesteading: the terraform era. The new idea: PERSISTENCE — what you shape STAYS shaped across runs (canals hold water run after run; the tile itself is a save file). Full-circle design: you dig canals and pan the FIRST WATER on a dead world — epoch 1's river, rebuilt by hand, generations later. Dust devils and feral terraformers (earlier Combine machines, still working, wrong planet) as enemies. Buildings: dome commons (tavern), canal works (sluice lineage's apotheosis), weather spires. Science: Areology / Arsenal (storm fence, terraform cannon) / Fabrication v9. Megaproject: the Generation Ark — and the transition is the saga's emotional peak: THE TOWN BOARDS THE SHIP. Every surviving townsfolk portrait, every generation's descendants, the Long Table — the town doesn't get left behind; it IS the ship (pillar 1, kept to the end). Engine: persistent tile-state mutation + save-scale.

### Epoch 10 — THE DEEP SKY (capstone)

## 4. The final milestone — THE CHARTER PRESS (the kids' contract builder)
**In fiction:** the Assay Office's final transformation — a printing press that stamps world-charters. **In reality:** the no-code adventure platform (VISION layers 1–3), grown up.
- **It does not wait for epoch 10.** The Press opens in BETA during epoch 2–3 (as soon as Town v1's contract board + the mode-manifest engine mature), with exactly two users: the kids. Every epoch then feeds it new verbs automatically — the builder grows WITH the game, ten content drops for free.
- **Layer 1 (beta):** describe an adventure in words at the tavern → the agent composes a mode-manifest + tile layout within validation contracts → playable, shareable as a file, posted to the family's board. Content only; the same two gates as crafted items (contract + sim/abuse validation); canon tone holds.
- **Layer 2:** conversational iteration with preview/approve ("faster bandits, bigger reward, night only") — permission-ladder pattern verbatim; diffs are visible before they apply.
- **Layer 3 (the milestone):** the full workshop — verb palette (every epoch's mechanics as composable pieces), tile picker across all era-stamped worlds, playbook-scripted actors (E7's recorder as the kids' scripting language: program by demonstration, no code), budget/validation meters visible like a build-mode HUD. NEW VERBS remain the adults' factory; kids request them and the factory ships them gated (the family IS the studio).
- **Sharing:** files first, forever fine; any public gallery is a separate future decision with real moderation questions (flagged, not designed).
- **Success metric (the gate the milestone ships against):** *a 12-year-old, unassisted, ships a playable adventure in under an hour that their sibling can't beat on the first try — and iterates it after losing an argument about fairness.*
