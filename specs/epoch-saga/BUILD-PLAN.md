## §0 THE SHAPE, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
The saga ships **one era at a time**, each as a complete playable loop (arrive → contracts → science ceiling → megaproject built under defense → ceremony → town transforms → next era's door), through the existing factory (attended specs → task masters → Codex lanes → fire drains → owner verdicts). Each era = ~10–16 gated slices across four workstreams (ENGINE / CONTENT / ART / CEREMONY+STORY). The storybook's 27 extra maps are **post-ship drip content** for their eras, never era-gates. Engine systems are built ONCE at their debut era and reused forever (the reuse spine is §4's backbone — by E7 an era is mostly content). E2 is the calibration era: it is already ~60% built, and its completion measures the factory's true era-pace before anyone promises dates.
