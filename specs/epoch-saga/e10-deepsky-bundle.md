# Epoch 10 "The Deep Sky" — design-locked capstone bundle · banked 2026-07-07

**Era thesis.** The Generation Ark sails the long dark with the whole saga aboard — every lineage's final form on one walkable ship, ten generations of portraits on the Long Table's wall. Outside: worlds to visit (procgen within authored families) and the last rival, **the Static** — entropy itself, the only enemy that was never greed. The final contracts don't extract; they PRESERVE. And the last science node opens the Charter Press: the town that built ten eras of machines finally builds the machine that builds worlds — and hands it to the children. Roll credits into the family's own editor.

**PALETTE NOTE:** *the full ten-era palette aboard the Ark (every deck keeps its era's grammar — walking the ship is walking the saga); space is deep ink with engraved star-stipple; nebulae are parchment-gold veils; THE STATIC is the anti-palette — desaturation itself, drawn as un-inked paper spreading — and defeating it re-inks the world (the game's whole visual thesis as a boss mechanic).*

## §A — ART (design-locked)
**The Ark (interior tile art):** deck sections per lineage — the Long Table mess (tavern's final form; the portrait wall IS the family's actual playthrough history, composited from their saved profiles), the Bridge School, the Charter Press hall (Assay lineage's endpoint: a great brass-and-teal press with tape-reels and a child-height lever — the lever is the point), the Pan Shrine (a small case: the original E1 pan; players who look close see it's the same sprite). Hull exterior sheet + engine-glow states.
**Visited-world biome family templates (3 to launch):** ember world (cooling lava veins — E2 palette echo) · glass steppe (E3 lightning-fused plains) · sea moon (E5 under alien tides) — each: atlas + props + ONE authored landmark set; layouts seeded (dungeon-forge graduated to worlds), contract-validated.
**Enemies (2+boss):** static-motes (drifting desaturation wisps that mute — literally silence — whatever they touch; audio design: they eat the mix channel by channel) · unraveled machines (any prior era's machine enemy, re-skinned as half-un-inked — the Static's converts; one mask treatment applies to existing sheets: ART EFFICIENCY, the whole rogue's gallery returns for free) · **BOSS: THE QUIET** — a storm-heart of pure Static: as it approaches, color drains outward from it in rings, the music thins to a heartbeat, YOUR OWN older-era weapons (which it "forgets" first) stop working in its aura — you finish the saga with the newest science defending the oldest things. Its defeat re-inks the world deck by deck, instrument by instrument. (Engine: desaturation aura shader + mix-channel ducking + weapon-era gating in a radius.)
**The Starlight Pan** (endgame weapon): pan sweep animation sheet — panning nebula-light into bolt charges; the E1 panning animation's exact timing, re-drawn in gold-on-ink.
**Townsfolk:** the whole living cast (aging pass final form) + the Charter-Keeper — canonically the PLAYER'S FAMILY's in-game presence: portraits generated from their profiles' history stats (procedural honor, not likeness). 
**Icons (10):** starlight, the Pan, static ward, re-ink, preserve-contract seal, world-charter, Ark, the Press, Quiet crest, the ten-era ribbon.
