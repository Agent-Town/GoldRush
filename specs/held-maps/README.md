## What this ladder is
The art campaigns (corrections 1 to 4, fidelity 1 and 2) were allowed to touch only what their own reviews had HELD for an art owner. Six maps came out of fidelity-2 untouched because their latest reviews hold nothing for art: Night Shift, Twin Banks, the Claim-Jumper Baron, the Trestle, the Glow Mesa and Relay Rush. Their holds belong to a camera, HUD, code, contract or layout owner. The camera and phone-HUD holds are item 1 of F-F2-39 (`tasks/sol-entry-framing-2.md`, `tasks/sol-phone-hud-entry-2.md`, running 2026-09-24); the code-owned presentation holds are item 3 (`tasks/sol-code-presentation-1.md`, queued behind the HUD task). This ladder is item 2: the holds that move a map's gameplay geometry or its start state, which no art run may touch.

## Laws (all slices)
1. **One map per master, one concern per slice.** A slice moves one map and ends in a playable checkpoint proven in a plain boot (no `?debug`) at 1280 and 390 beside the map's plate. The plate is the reference, not a pixel target (owner, the F-CORR4-2 table: "the plates were reference, the maps play").
2. **Geometry is declared, never painted.** A change lands in the contract data first (`assets/contracts/<epoch>/contracts.json`; the store's pilot contracts for terrain truth) and the render follows it (`src/world/Terrain.ts` reads the water block, `BuildSystem` the pre-placed fixtures). No slice changes a sim rule through render code. Economy stays the only gold writer and CombatSystem the only damage resolver.
3. **The price of moving geometry is paid in the same master, and listed:** the map's null floors re-recorded (`node scripts/null-floor-anchors.mjs`, then `--check`), every fixture and test constant that pins the map's start state re-minted with the reason written down (the E1 mechanics-manifest fixture `e2e/fixtures/e1-mechanics-manifests.json` only if the manifest actually moves), and a same-era pin whose cause names the contract change, measured last on the merged tree. Never an era bump (owner rule). Stored reels of the map on the county board keep their own pin's verdict and read as having ridden an earlier pin; the 2026-09-13 word allows that before players arrive.
4. **The First-Claim Law stands.** The Claim is not touched by any slice here; a slice moves only the map it names, and sibling maps of the same tile family keep their numbers (a per-map declaration, never a family default).
5. **Art follows geometry.** Where a declaration leaves the sculpt wrong (a narrower river band over a wide bed, a raised level under a flat mesh), the same master re-cuts the store's sources (`.blend` in `Agent-Town/GoldRush-assets`) as edits of the existing art, on a store branch, landed on the store's main before the pin. Astra in lane-c owns those slices; a data-only slice may run as an Opus implementer in a scratch worktree.
6. **The hero and the agents still play.** Every slice re-runs the walk probes of `tasks/f-corr4-2-variant-footprints.md` item 3 on its map (spawn not inside a blocker, every station, stake, site and route point reachable on foot) and the same-game audit; the release-physical laws of `specs/release-e1/README.md` and the E1 first-town payload law (52,000,000 B) hold for E1 maps; task-025 holds everywhere (bandits cross water only at a ford).
7. **Standing firewalls:** the light ramp waves (dusk 5, dark 10, dawn 25), the secure waves, the enemy rosters, the agent tool surface and its verbs, the county door and the assayer are untouched by this ladder.

### HM-01a The lanterns: a lit home bank, a cold far bank (the queueable slice)
**The design.** The three posts on the home bank, the ones that flank the Lampworks yard mount at (8, 18) and the stake, start lit: (0, 16), (-16, 18), (16, 18) declared without `wrecked`, full HP 35, their `relightCost` 8 kept for the day a wrecker puts one out. The relight economy keeps four posts and every post the wreckers put out.

**Why this and not the plate's seven.** All seven lit at entry needs a rule that puts some of them out again, or the relight verb, the manifest's interactable and the map's rules text ("Relight cold lanterns or build new posts to see threats.") lose their object. That rule would be new simulation (a scripted snuff at the dusk keyframe, event-logged in both engines); it is written below as option (b) for the owner, not recommended for the first step. Leaving all seven cold is the state the review refused to fake.

**Options for the owner.**
