# Loot, Builds & the Endgame Dial — the replayability dimension (SEED SPEC)
Status: **SEED — banked 2026-07-08 ~11:00, owner-gated, explicitly NOT-NOW** (owner: "not for now but potentially later… Just thinking ahead here."). Zero factory time until his word. This document exists so the thinking survives.

## The owner's vision (verbatim anchors)
"What if heroes could have an inventory and use items? These items could drop from bandits/monsters and the bosses could give better loot. Could also be 'construction blueprints' or other items that are helpful to develop the story. Then the players could also spec their weapons with weapon spec trees. That allows for more replayability. I am inspired a bit here by Minecraft Dungeons, Deep Rock Galactica Survivor or the Diablo series. The combination of the right items and the right skills allowed to have massive depth and replayability. We could then later also allow user to set the difficulty of the contracts and thus increase the damage and health massively for the end game while giving better loot."

## The four pillars
1. **INVENTORY & ITEMS**: hero gear slots (few, ledger-styled — tool/charm/boots class), items DROP from bandits (rare), elites (uncommon), bosses (guaranteed, better). Items are frontier-canon (a weighted pan, tempered boot-soles, a surveyor's charm — never generic +5 swords).
2. **BLUEPRINTS AS STORY LOOT**: drops that unlock CONTENT, not just stats — construction blueprints (a building variant, a megaproject shortcut stage), contract maps (unlock a hidden contract), story letters (beat-chains). **PRECEDENT ALREADY LIVE: the Baron's Rocket Cart capture unlocking E2's Sky-Rocket node IS the first blueprint drop** — the pattern works; this pillar generalizes it.
3. **WEAPON SPEC TREES**: per-weapon mini-trees (Spark Rig / Blast Charges / Sky-Rocket Battery / later era weapons) — persistent meta-trees (like research) or per-run (like upgrades)? DESIGN FORK for ratification. SCI-02's family/mastery machinery = the natural substrate (mastery already tracks per-family usage!).
4. **THE ENDGAME DIAL**: per-contract difficulty setting (extends the existing greenhorn/trail/vein-hunter presets) scaling enemy HP/damage steeply ("massively for the end game") with loot quality scaling to match — the Diablo-torment pattern. Board cards show dial + best-at-dial. Feeds the replay loop: better loot → higher dials → better loot.
