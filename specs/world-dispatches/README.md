## The law
World-state backstory REVEALS THROUGH PROGRESS — milestones unlock dispatches; nothing is dumped up front. The outside world's fall to the Gold Fever (and the town's growing exception) arrives in fragments the player earns.

## The three vehicles (all existing systems — no new engine)
1. **MEI DELIVERS (the newsie is the mechanism)**: Mei's plaza barks gain a progress-gated pool of WORLD lines — one new dispatch per milestone (first Baron defeat, each era activation, each megaproject raise). One sentence each, ledger voice: "Papers say the coast towns pan through the night now and don't know why." She is literally the newsie; the world arrives with her.
2. **THE WORLD OUTSIDE (ledger pages)**: each era chapter of the Claim Ledger (era-chapters slice, queued) reserves one entry — "The World Outside, Era N" — locked until the era is REACHED, filled with 3-5 dispatch fragments: where the Fever has spread, what the Baron builds, who still writes letters. Discovered like any entry; re-readable; the saga's history book assembles as you climb.
3. **CEREMONY POSTSCRIPTS**: each era-activation ceremony gains ONE closing line about the world ("The whistle carries down the valley. Somewhere past the hills, the Baron hears it.") — the biggest moments carry the biggest fragments.

## Voice & canon laws
Ledger voice, warm, never grim-dark (canon §9); the Fevered are victims; the Baron is the author of the madness; the town's tone is stubborn hope, not dread. Every dispatch cites its era in the lore wiki when authored (content lands in lore/world-dispatches.md as the single source, entries reference it).
