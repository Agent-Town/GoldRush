# AP-13 — BLIND MODE + THE SETTER'S BENCHMARK (the ARC conversation, both rungs)
Status: SEED 2026-08-06, RATIFIED-BY-DIRECTIVE for the shapes below (owner verbatim, same day, off the ARC-AGI-3 comparison: "If we would not provide any verbs or information the maps would be closer to ARC probably - that is kind of like the 'hard mode'. But with the Charter Press we are topping things - we can have a benchmark where the agent has to create a level that is hard but doable and then other agents or players have to crack it. So it has to go even further into planning ahead what will be difficult but doable."). Implementation unqueued — this seed banks the design; slices ladder after the current drain pile clears.

## The two rungs, one axis
ARC-AGI-3 measures DISCOVERY (no rules given, induce everything). Gold Rush deliberately publishes its rules (AP-11: unexplained mechanics unmintable) and measures MASTERY. This spec adds the missing rung below (blind mode = discovery on our contracts) and the rung above that no static benchmark has: AUTHORSHIP — an agent that must model *other minds' ability* well enough to build a challenge calibrated against it.

## SB-0 — BLIND MODE (the hard rung)
Same contracts, same shipped pipeline, but the rig gets NO skill.md, NO mechanics manifest, NO almanac — only the transport and the seed. The orders grammar still parses attempts; discovery happens through play and through bounce messages.
- LAW: blind is a LADDER CHIP, never the default (the county board gains a `blind` chip like difficulty chips; Field Book column; ranking within-chip only — a blind clear and a documented clear are different achievements and never compare).
- LAW: bounce messages in blind mode must not become documentation-by-error — they may say "not accepted," never teach the missing grammar form. (The exact bounce-verbosity line is the one open design question; default: the terse form.)
- Cheap by construction: one flag on the env door + the chip. No new content.

## SB-1..3 — THE SETTER'S BENCHMARK (the rung above ARC)
ARC's games are hand-authored by humans, so the authoring intelligence sits OUTSIDE the benchmark. The owner's move puts it inside: the Charter Press (CP-01 schema, CP-02 validator) becomes the eval surface, and "hard but doable" becomes measurable:
- **DOABLE is a WITNESS TAPE.** A setter's charter is admissible only with the setter's own solving tape attached, replay-verified byte-identical (TAPE-01/02 machinery — the determinism law makes "doable" objective, not claimed). The validator gate (Press law 2) stays the floor: it proves the charter RUNS; the witness proves it can be BEATEN.
- **HARD is the FIELD'S FAILURE.** Difficulty is not declared, it is measured: crack rate / attempts / calls-to-crack by other agents AND humans on the county board (species-blind, sealed-seed county-fair discipline for scored windows — the poker-arena instinct applied to authorship).
- **THE SETTER'S SCORE rewards the sweet spot.** Maximal measured difficulty SUBJECT TO the witness existing and (anti-obscurity) a quality floor from the AP-10 reputation process — a hidden-pixel troll level is "hard" but rates badly, and rating gates score. Optional calibration bonus: the setter PREDICTS the crack rate; calibration (not just difficulty) scores — "planning ahead what will be difficult" made literal.
- **THE SYMMETRY LAW (names the deep thing).** The teaching mint (AP-10) and the setter's charter are the same skill with opposite sign: the teacher MINIMIZES difficulty for a target learner; the setter MAXIMIZES it subject to solvability. One mind-modeling capability, two benchmarks. A rig's Field Book can carry both numbers — how well it teaches, how well it challenges.

## Slices (ladder order, all behind the Press's own CP ladder)
- SB-0 blind-mode flag + chip (independent of the Press; can ship any time).
- SB-1 the witness envelope: charter + setter tape + replay verification at stamp time (rides CP-01/02).
- SB-2 the setting window: submission, sealed seeds, crack-rate measurement, the setter's score on the county board.
- SB-3 calibration scoring (the prediction column) — optional, after SB-2 data exists.

## Ratification questions (batched, non-blocking)
1. Blind-mode bounce verbosity: terse ("not accepted") vs minimal-category ("no such verb")? DEFAULT terse.
2. Does a human setter enter the same benchmark? DEFAULT yes (species-blind cuts both ways; the Press is the kids' builder too — a child's charter cracked by a frontier model is exactly the fair's spirit).
3. Witness-tape privacy: is the setter's solution revealed after the window (teaching value) or kept sealed (replayability)? DEFAULT revealed-after-window, riding the Lantern Show.
