## PART A — the ten undesigned mechanics (design → build on ratification)

**A1 · e3-fairground — the crowd flocks.** Declared: `crowdFlocks {count:3, escortRadius:7}`; wheel/pavilions/powerGrid/dayNight ALL already live (FerrisWheel.ts complete; the flocks are the only hole). DESIGN: three festival flocks cross the midway (south gate → the wheel plaza and back), one crossing per night cycle; a flock takes fright if an enemy enters its escort radius (7) — frightened flocks scatter home and the crossing fails. Objective: all three flocks complete a crossing AND the wheel is spinning at secureWave 12. Headless: flock entities in the sim tick; agent protects with ordinary combat/positioning. DEFAULTS: flock speed = hero walk × 0.6; scatter resets next night.

**A2 · e5-stillwater — the noise-hunt.** Declared: fogZone (whole map), 2 quietZones, 3 noiseSources (air-pump r18, engine r24, harpoon-reload r14), machine_leviathan roster (depth-travel, both edges). DESIGN: a NOISE meter — each declared source emits while its machine runs (pump = auto-pan active, engine = boat moves, harpoon = turret volley); the leviathan holds a trail toward the loudest emitter above threshold and strikes it (building damage; it never one-shots the hero), losing the trail after N quiet seconds; quiet zones let you harvest/drift silently (hand-pan = harvest without pump noise). Fog: permanent visibility shroud (watch radius capped; render haze). Secure = survive to wave 12 managing noise. DEFAULTS: trail-hold 8s of quiet to shake; leviathan damage tuned to threaten machines, not instakill.

**A3 · e7-echo-canyon — the broadcast mirror.** Declared: `broadcastMirror {delay:"next-wave"}` + map-wide mirror zone; teachingIntent "your habits have a shadow." DESIGN: every playbook the player/agent USES this run is recorded; the NEXT wave fields one corrupted copy per use — a mirrored enemy squad (data_rustler variant, tinted) whose composition echoes the playbook's own shape (e.g. a turret-volley playbook returns as a ranged squad). No playbook use → no mirrors. DEFAULTS: cap 3 mirrored squads/wave; mirrors carry +10% hp per repeat of the SAME playbook (habit punished, variety rewarded — the declared lesson).

**A4 · e7-dead-band — the signal suppression.** Declared: `signalSuppression {drones:false, playbooks:false, relayChains:false}` (literal-false schema; map-wide null zone). DESIGN: a contract-level OFF switch on the three named systems — the drone seam already exists (`droneCanOperate`), playbook record/use refuses with the county voice ("the Dead Band swallows it"), relay chains never link. That's the whole mechanic: nostalgia by subtraction, exactly as declared. No parameters to choose.

**A5 · e7-relay-rush — the interference front.** Declared: front "crosses the map on a schedule and mutes everything it swallows," `relayTarget:"N"` (a PLACEHOLDER — the real number was never authored), four 10×10 relay-site buildZones on the north ridge. DESIGN: a vertical static wall sweeps west→east on a fixed cadence; anything it covers is MUTED (buildings offline, playbooks/drones dead) until it passes; objective = have N relay sites LIT (a powered building standing on the site) before the Nth front arrives, then survive to secure. DEFAULTS: **N = 3 of 4**; front cadence 90s, crossing time 20s; muted ≠ damaged.

**A6 · e8-far-side — the crossing and the probe.** Declared: map-wide signalSuppression (reuse A4's consumer — same schema), suit-only vacuum (flavor + the existing `vacuum` flag), probeRecoveryZone crater, `probePlayback {trigger:"probe-recovered"}`. DESIGN: A4's suppression consumer runs here too (drones+playbooks off); objective = reach the probe crater and RECOVER (a context action in the zone), which fires the one-time playback — the E7 jack-board's banked "wrong-number hello" fragment (the cross-era payoff already written in E7SignalSystem) — then hold to secure. Suit-only stays visual/vacuum-flag (nothing more is declared).

**A7 · e8-low-orbit — momentum is commitment.** Declared: zeroGravity (free-fall movement + `projectiles:"orbital-return"` — COMPUTED today, consumed by NOTHING), handhold spine route, two debris fields, scaffold zones = the three buildZones. DESIGN: (1) orbital return — a missed lob re-enters after T seconds at its original vector's continuation (one return, then gone); (2) handholds — SOFT constraint: on-route/on-scaffold movement is normal, off-route drifts (thrust response halves, momentum carries — the declared lesson), never a hard wall; (3) debris fields — slow+minor-damage hazard bands. DEFAULTS: return T = 12s; drift penalty 0.5× control.

**A8 · e9-seed-run — plant the future.** Declared: persistentPlanting; caravan escort in the objective prose; three plant-ground stakes; TileStateStore ALREADY has the exact mechanic rehearsed (green-waypoint no-spawn discs, profile-persistent, staged-at-run-end — currently debug-gated on dry-gulch). DESIGN: a seed-vault caravan crosses south→north (escort survival like the declared lesson); at each of the three grounds the player/agent may PLANT a vault (context action) — planting permanently creates a green waypoint (no-spawn disc) for ALL future runs on this map, but each planted vault weakens the caravan's remaining guard (spend now, shelter forever). Objective: caravan reaches the basin. Headless: TileStateStore is already threaded into E9's socket family.

**A9 · e9-devils-alley — the wind replans.** Declared: scheduledRelocation ("dust devils cross on schedules and relocate unanchored buildings instead of destroying them"); THREE patrol routes authored (the devil sweeps, z=-26/0/+26); the dust-devil entity already exists with a live position (E9CanalSystem, dome-basin). DESIGN: devils sweep the three routes on a schedule; a building in a devil's path is PICKED UP and DROPPED at the sweep's end point, alive (played for laughs — the declared tone); buildings inside the three anchor-bay buildZones are ANCHORED and immune. DEFAULTS: one sweep per wave, alternating routes; relocation never damages.

**A10 · e9-old-canal — inheritance is edited.** Declared: persistentCanalChoices ("each segment permanently remembers re-dig or demolish; the final water route follows the combined choices"); three decision stakes on three canal-segment buildZones; E9CanalSystem (wet/dry state) + TileStateStore both live in the epoch. DESIGN: each segment offers a one-time persistent CHOICE at its stake (context action): RE-DIG → the segment permanently carries water on all future runs (a wet canal band: water source + the canal system's benefits) · DEMOLISH → permanently open build ground. The final flow renders from the combined persisted choices. Objective: decide all three + survive to secure.
