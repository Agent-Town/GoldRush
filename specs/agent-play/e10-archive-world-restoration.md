## 3. The design (defaults ratifiable by silence)
**The loop: carry light into a dead wing, hold it through the squall, and the wing remembers itself.**
- **Light-hold consumer** (keyed on a new `twist.archiveWorld.lightHold` field, F-1471-1): a hold site is LIT while a powered light-emitting building (beacon class — the existing beacon building is the default emitter) stands inside its radius-4 disc. A wing RE-INKS when its site stays lit through one complete squall (telegraph + squall phases, uninterrupted). Wings re-ink IN DECLARED ORDER (1→2→3): later sites accept a beacon but cannot re-ink before their predecessors (the county voice explains — one line, warm).
- **Squalls**: identical scheduler family to Ember Shore (`specs/agent-play/e10-ember-shore-preserve.md` §3) — calm 60s / telegraph 8s / squall 25s; during a squall, mote pressure concentrates on LIT sites (the Static wants the light out — the mutation law's final shape: it craves the shaped signal of care). If a site's beacon is destroyed or unpowered mid-squall, the hold fails and restarts next squall; the wing is never damaged — losing here looks like NOTHING happening, per the teaching intent.
- **Re-ink presentation** (browser): a re-inked wing's zone lifts from desaturated to full era palette (tile tint swap at zone granularity is enough at launch; the full re-ink shader is finale tech). Headless: wing states in snapshots.
- **Persistent lore-unlock**: at RUN END (win or loss), wings re-inked this run stage into TileStateStore: permanently re-inked on ALL future runs of this map, and each unlocks one LORE PAGE (§6) — surfaced in the claim ledger (the owner ruled stats/records belong "into the claim ledger to be honest", 2026-08-15; lore pages join that surface) and written into `lore/` per the lore law. A fully re-inked archive (all three) additionally re-inks the "ours, unless." shelf's pictogram frame — no page, just the shelf no longer empty. The question it asks is the point.
- **Roster**: `static_mote` (orbit lit sites) + `unraveled_machine` (chase; mask source e6 feral_toaster at launch, same PROPOSAL seam as Ember Shore). No boss — the Quiet is the finale's.
- **Anchors**: 4 harvestAnchors in the archive-entry south zone (dust-pan the entry hall; the wings themselves yield nothing — you are not here to extract).
- **Secure**: `twist.secureWave: 12`; objective latch = at least ONE wing re-inked this run (progression beyond that is the player's ambition and the persistence's job, not the gate's).

## 6. The three lore pages (PROPOSAL — owner reads, corrects, or blesses; drafted to STORYBOOK canon)
- **Wing 1, west stacks — "The Catalog of Answered Doors."** A shipping ledger from the old world's catalog civilization (the warehouse E4 found; the sea-floor prototypes E5 found): page after page of orders FILLED — and the last column, delivery confirmations, goes quiet mid-page. No catastrophe recorded. The orders simply stop being acknowledged. The final entry is stamped with the old world's own pictogram for "answer pending." (Canon anchors: the wider world is old, `STORYBOOK.md:336`; the world stopped answering, `:418`.)
- **Wing 2, east stacks — "The Minutes of the Last Standards Body."** A committee's minutes, decades apart: first arguing gauge widths and signal codes with joyful pedantry; then arguing attendance; then a single secretary recording that no delegates came, moving that the standard be preserved as written, seconding her own motion, adjourning. The page is initialed twice by the same hand. (Tone anchor: warm law — no wreckage described, absence carries it.)
- **Wing 3, warning shelf — "The Instruction That Was Followed."** The old world's own recall notice — their "COME HOME," issued generations before the town's (the House's second imperative, `STORYBOOK.md:418`) — with distribution marks showing it was received everywhere and a homecoming registry that was never filled in. Beneath it, in a different, later hand, the pictogram sequence the town reads as: *kept for whoever asks next.* This page is the map's thesis and the era's warning, shown never said.
