# COSMETICS — THE TAILOR'S WAGON & THE WARDROBE
STATUS: RATIFIED-V1 2026-07-26 (owner, verbatim: "we will then need something like a 'weardrobe house' or a 'tailor' and the user can enter that house and switch skins for the Prospector and the player? Maybe we could even have sets for the ingame items as well.")

## Laws
1. COSMETIC-ONLY, forever: skins never touch stats, hitboxes, or readability classes (silhouette stays honest at gameplay zoom).
2. Skins are profile data; grants are earned in-world (the desk pays coats; prizes redeem stubs); no store, no currency in E1.
3. Placeholder-first: binding is by name with stock fallback — art lands whenever.

## V1 (release): THE TAILOR'S WAGON
- A tailor's wagon parks on the plaza for the testing era (the newsie/desk pattern: a prop + door, not a new building — reuse the shipped wagon body + a hung cloth sign, render-side).
- The door opens THE WARDROBE (house UI): two racks — **The Prospector** (stock · Complainant's Coat · Gilded) and **The Partner** (the heroine: stock · the Claim-Day Neckerchief). Equip per profile; the reporter grant is a SET (coat + neckerchief together — the county dresses both of you).
- Settings keeps no picker (the wagon IS the surface; one door, in-world).

## V2+ (banked, post-release): ITEM SETS — dress packs for the works themselves (sluice/turret/palisade/lantern skins as era-flavored sets, earned by era completion; the wardrobe gains a third rack). Design only; no slice until the owner calls it.

## THE WALKING LAW (owner-confirmed 2026-07-28: "each cosmetic that we introduce will result into a different animation for the character that uses it, right? That is kind of the idea behind cosmetics.")
A cosmetic is a RE-DRESSED ACTOR, not a texture swap. No cosmetic ships until its wearer's FULL sheet set exists in that dress: every convention the base wearer has (hover4-a/b, hover8, walk4/8...) and — post EIGHT WINDS — the diagonal siblings too. The stock fallback (placeholder-first) may bridge, but the wardrobe UI marks an incompletely-sheeted skin as "at the tailor's" rather than equipping a skin that breaks direction coverage.
COST CONTAINMENT, so the multiplier stays countable: (1) the wardrobe is CURATED and EARNED — racks grow by owner call, never by generator enthusiasm; (2) item sets (V2) are 3D material/mesh variants — zero sheets, the multiplication never touches them; (3) each new skin's price is stated at proposal time: base-sheet-count × (1 + diagonals). Current bill from this law: Prospector coats ×3 need diagonal hover siblings (EIGHT WINDS tail); the Partner's Claim-Day Neckerchief art (not yet landed) ships as a full set from day one.
