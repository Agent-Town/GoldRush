Status: SEED 2026-07-11 (owner testimony, verbatim: "in the later epochs there would be other currencies than gold and that there should be exchange possible. ## THE THREE TIERS (the membrane law: value flows DOWN-tier only as cosmetics, never up-tier as power)
1. **RUN CURRENCIES (sim, deterministic):** gold (E1's), then era resources per bundle — steam-era coal/pressure, E4 FUEL + TAR (already art-canon: "the era's two products"), E9 WATER ("after nine eras, water"). Exchange between run currencies = GAMEPLAY, at the Bank building, rates data-driven per epoch manifest (the 077 substrate carries them; deterministic, sim-side, shared-pot in MP).
2. **THE META CURRENCY (earned, never bought):** proposed name **SCRIP** (frontier-canonical: company scrip → town scrip). Earned by play across runs (contract results, medals, era ceremonies). Spends on IDENTITY: town cosmetics, facade variants, monument skins, outfit dyes, Commons decorations. NEVER purchasable with money/token (that's what keeps it a badge of play, not a wallet).
3. **THE TOKEN (outside the game's economy, per the ratified spec):** buys cosmetics/patronage via the two-door patron model. May unlock the SAME cosmetic CLASS as scrip (parallel doors: play earns it or patronage buys it — both cosmetic-only) but NEVER converts to scrip, gold, or any run currency. No exchange desk between tier 3 and tiers 1–2 exists, ever — that absence IS the design.

## THE BANK (the building lineage; heritage-consistent)
E2/E3: **the Assay Bank** joins the growth roster (the assay office's sibling — weighing was always proto-banking): in-run era-currency exchange counter + the scrip ledger book (meta balance, in-world voice). → E4-E6: vault, notes, the works. → **E7: THE EXCHANGE (the painted plate exists — plate-e7-bld-the-exchange.png): the bank's apotheosis in the signal era**, wired markets, rate boards by pictogram. The Family-Business law applies: the Bank arrives WITH its banker family. Household seed [LATER].
The token/patronage door is NOT in the Bank and not in-run at all — it lives web-side/Commons-side per the token spec (kids-never-see-wallet law holds structurally).
