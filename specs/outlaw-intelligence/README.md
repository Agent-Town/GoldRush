# OUTLAW INTELLIGENCE — they want the claim, not your flesh
STATUS: DRAFT 2026-07-13 — awaiting owner ratification. Owner insight, verbatim: "The thing that suddenly feels a bit weird to me is that the opponents don't also use weapons or are smarter. Somehow it feels more like these are Zombies? I did not realise this earlier."

## The diagnosis
The survivors genre ships zombie-wave DNA: bodies path at the player, contact damage, done. Our fiction promises OUTLAWS — people and machines with intent (ADR-001: frontier-tech, NEVER firearms; enemies are outlaws/companies/machines/nature, never mindless). The thief (steals, flees), the wrecker (hunts buildings), and the Baron (rockets, agenda) already break the mold; the base crowd doesn't. This spec turns the crowd from hungry bodies into a raiding party.

## The four moves (each independently shippable, cheapest first)

### A. INTENT — the claim is the target (the identity move, recommended core)
Most base outlaws stop targeting the hero. Their goal ladder: reachable GOLD (stockpile skims, sluice tampering, seam poaching — thief-lite grabs with small carry amounts) → else pressure the workings → fight the hero ONLY when intercepted or cornered. The player's job flips from "survive the wave" to "defend the claim" — which is the game's actual name and promise. Hero pursuit remains for a minority (guards/brutes) so combat pressure never vanishes.
- Cost: retargeting logic on the existing thief/wrecker chassis + carry/drop rules. No new art required to START (same sprites, new intent reads through behavior).
- Risk: economy pressure needs tuning (steal rates vs current wave damage pressure).

### B. FRONTIER-TECH ARMS — a ranged tier, ADR-001-safe (flavor per era)
A minority of each era's roster gets a telegraphed ranged tool — never a gun:
- E1: thrown DYNAMITE STICK (slow arc, fuse sparkle, dodgeable AoE), BOLA/LASSO SNARE (brief hero slow — pairs with the night-lantern fiction).
- E2: steam-pressure SPITTER (machine, straight hissing bolt), coal-smoke POT (vision soft-denial pocket).
- E3+: arc-static casters, tram-rail sappers (era bundles already name candidates).
Budget: 1-2 ranged variants per era, small spawn share (10-20%), loud telegraphs (readability law).

### C. MORALE & CRAFT — reactive smarts (pairs with gap-flow, recommended core)
- FLINCH/BREAK: badly hurt outlaws disengage, regroup at range, re-approach in a group (zombies never retreat; people do).
- LANE LEARNING: after N deaths in one kill-lane inside a wave, later spawns of that wave bias to a DIFFERENT gap (they saw the bodies — builds directly on gap-flow's wall-following).
- CORNERED RULE: intent-A outlaws caught by the hero fight back harder briefly (no free farming of fleeing thieves).

### D. THE ERA INTELLIGENCE LADDER (the saga arc, slow burn)
Intelligence as era fiction: E1 rabble (A+C only) → E2 company crews (visible ROLES: a foreman buffs/directs, crews split by assignment) → E3 machine patterns (readable, exploitable cycles) → later eras per their bundles. Each era bundle gains an "intelligence" line the way it has an arsenal line.

## Laws
1. ADR-001 absolute: no firearms, ever. Ranged = thrown/steam/arc/snare tools with visible arcs and telegraphs.
2. Readability outranks cleverness: every new behavior needs a visible tell (pose, carried tool, tint) — the wrecker-tell pattern.
3. The sim stays planar/deterministic; all of this is target/steering/state logic on the existing enemy chassis.
4. Difficulty identity holds: smarter ≠ harder-stat-checks. Greenhorn tuning keeps outcomes gentle even when behavior reads smart.
5. Rollout: nothing lands before gap-flow merges (C builds on it); A ships behind a per-contract flag for one owner playtest before default-on.

## Ratification questions (owner)
1. Is INTENT-A the identity you want (defend the claim, not yourself)? It is the biggest feel-shift in the stack.
2. Ranged share: does 10-20% per wave feel right, or keep E1 purely melee and start ranged at E2 (companies bring tools)?
3. Morale: should base outlaws flee at low HP (full anti-zombie) or only disengage-and-regroup (keeps wave pressure)?
4. Sequencing: A+C first (behavior, no art), then B per era with its art batch?
