## 2. The bars each sheet was judged against

| bar | how it was measured | tool |
|---|---|---|
| grid regularity | true grid inferred from full-height/full-width background gutters, compared to the declared grid; every internal cut probed for content crossing it (`bleedPx`) and for clean gutter width | `anim-pass-inspect.mjs` |
| `#ff00ff` purity | background fraction within tol 26 of the detected key; halo band (tol..90) as a share of the sheet; interior spill blobs not reachable from the border | `anim-pass-inspect.mjs` |
| direction-row correctness | normalized 16×16 silhouette per cell; per-row self-mirror (cardinal views are near-symmetric, profiles are not); row-pair direct vs mirrored distance — **then confirmed at head magnification** | `anim-pass-facing.mjs` + `anim-pass-heads.mjs` |
| frame distinctness | pairwise hash screen, then bbox-aligned full-resolution mean \|ΔRGB\| and >12/255 difference share, direct and flipped | `anim-pass-dupecheck.mjs` |
| walk-cycle continuity | per-row series of figure height, baseline, centroid, foot span and foot-run count (2 runs = contact, 1 = passing) with their spreads | `anim-pass-inspect.mjs` |
| era/character fidelity | read against the sheet's own cast note and the surrounding sheets | eyes |

A note on honesty: the VERDICT column marks each row **(eye)** or **(measured)**. "(eye)" means I opened the art and looked at it. "(measured)" means it passed every numeric bar and I did **not** individually eyeball it — true of most of the 58 CLEAN sheets. Nothing in this file claims a visual judgement that was not made.

Two metrics were computed, examined, and then **deliberately dropped from the verdict** because they fire on legitimate art: enclosed background "holes" (the gap between an arm and a torso is an enclosed pocket, and the extractor keys it on purpose), and bleed under 40 px (antialiasing on the cut, absorbed by bbox-centring). They remain in the per-sheet JSON.

## M3.0 — the tail was mis-stated, in both directions

The task briefed "the 7 remaining LIVE-BOUND MENDs — all sub-pixel sliver cell-cut artifacts" and "~22 non-live MENDs then the 7 REGENERATE verdicts". §3's own tables say otherwise, and the tables are right:

Then measurement halved it again. **A `bleedPx` probe cannot see what it is famous for.** `probeBoundaries` counts content sitting *on* the cut line (`anim-pass-inspect.mjs:147-155`) — one pixel wide. That fires identically for a neighbour's limb genuinely CROSSING the cut and for a figure merely touching its OWN cell's first scanline. The first is pollution; the second is nothing at all.

The instrument that separates them is `scripts/anim-pass-cut.mjs` (new): 8-connected components over the whole sheet, each assigned to the cell holding the plurality of its mass, with the out-of-cell remainder reported as foreign. It also refuses to guess — a component straddling a cut with a *large* minority is two figures drawn touching, and deleting the minority would delete a figure, so those are reported as STRADDLE and never auto-erased. **Across all 14 sheets: 0 STRADDLEs. Every real defect was a clean OVERHANG, minority mass 0.04–3.05%.**

One more correction the probe forced: it measures cuts at `Math.round(c * w/cols)`, but `extract-alpha` slices at `floor(w/cols)` (`:292`). On a 1254px sheet at 4 columns those differ by up to 2px. All M3 geometry uses extract-alpha's, because that is what ships.

## M3.1 — why the mend is lossless, and why the scale is pinned

Two properties made this safe to do at all, and both are in the extractor:

- **Lossless.** Cell sampling is clamped to the cell rect (`extract-alpha.mjs:324,328`), so a figure's pixels lying outside its own cell were *already* dropped from its shipped frame. Erasing them removes nothing the owner ships and stops them polluting the neighbour, whose bbox they were inflating.
- **Scale had to be pinned.** `extract-alpha` derives ONE shared scale from the largest bbox on the sheet (`:310-311`) — and on three live sheets that largest bbox **was the pollution**. Letting it re-fit after the mend measured **+10.5% / +14.1% / +16.0%** on every figure. That is a gameplay-visible resize, not a mend. Scale is pinned to the shipped value on every sheet; all 7 came back unchanged.

Recovering that 10–16% dead margin is a real improvement and an **owner call**, not a side effect of a defect fix.

**This is the largest finding of the pass and it was not on anyone's list.**

Every keyed character cell carries semi-transparent `#ff00ff` on its silhouette edge: **338,575 px across 65 sheets / 1051 cells, 1.66% of all figure pixels**, worst `char-hero-sheet-walk8` at 5.99%. The tell that it is not art: of those 338,575 pixels, **zero are fully opaque**, and 99.97% are adjacent to a transparent pixel. Real art is opaque somewhere.

**Mechanism.** `extract-alpha` keys the sheet by setting ALPHA to 0 while leaving the background's RGB at pure magenta. `sliceGrid` then resamples each cell with a bilinear filter that averages all four channels *independently* (`:331-337`). Straight (non-premultiplied) RGB averaging across a silhouette edge mixes the figure's colour with `#ff00ff` in proportion to **area, not alpha** — so every edge pixel picks up magenta. Despill cannot save it: it runs *before* the slice and skips alpha-0 pixels (`:245`), so the key is still pure magenta when the filter reads it.

**The fix is proven, not proposed.** `scripts/anim-pass-fringe.mjs` re-implements the bake; its `--control` output is **byte-identical to `extract-alpha` on all 32 baron cells**, which is what makes the treatment arm trustworthy. Changing exactly one thing — weight the colour average by alpha, then un-premultiply — takes the fringe **6088 → 0** on the same sheet. By eye: `crops/m3-fringe-control-vs-fixed.png`, pale rim left, clean silhouette right, figure otherwise unchanged.

**Not applied here.** `extract-alpha.mjs` is shared pipeline, outside this claim, and the fix re-bakes all 462 shipped cells — it wants its own lane task, its own gate run and a deliberate re-bake. **Recommended as the next art-pipeline item; it improves every character sprite in the game in one change.**
