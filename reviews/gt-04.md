## What it does
Turrets/beacons/hero Spark Rig gain terrain line-of-sight and high-ground range
when the active claim has elevation tiles (`hasElevationTile()` gate — no-op on
flat claims). Ridges block bolt acquisition from both sides; turret lobs still arc
over (palisade firing-line OR LoS). High ground extends effective range by
`Balance.gt.highGroundRangeBonus` (1.5×). Adds `terrainLineOfSight` / `highGroundRange`
in `sim/TileHeight`, threads an optional `effectiveRange` through `TargetingSystem.findNearest`
(backward-compatible: absent → `targetRange = range`, identical filter to before), and
surfaces `terrain.gt` / `terrain.lastLos` + combat `shots`/`lastShotKind`/`lastShotOwnerId`
in the diagnostics types.
