## What it does
Adds a headless **empirical** crafted-item validator that closes the slice-03 gap: the slice-01 PAPER validator (`CraftingQueueContract`) only checks an item's *declared* stat budget, so it approves an item whose declared numbers fit the cap but whose *actual* in-sim effect over-performs. `StatSimHarness.ts` runs a typed `CraftedItemDef` in **seeded, scripted, deterministic** sim windows and measures real DPS / survival / gold-rate deltas vs a no-item baseline, then emits a verdict (`within-tolerance` vs `overperforming`) in the 033 approved/rejected fixture shape. Entry is `?debug&simitem=…`-gated (dynamic `import()` → its own 7 kB chunk); `window.__GR_STAT_SIM__` is the diagnostics seam. **Zero effect on normal play** — the import block in `main.ts` is skipped unless BOTH `debug` and `simitem` params are present.
