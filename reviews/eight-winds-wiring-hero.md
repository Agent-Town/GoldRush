## Findings

### F-1176-1 — THE DEFECT IS A CLASS OF 26 CELLS ACROSS TWO SHEETS, NOT 9 ON THE HERO. ✓ VERIFIED THREE WAYS.

The runner measured its own scope and reported 9 stale hero masters. That is correct and
**exactly reproduced** — but it is a sample, not the class, and the sheet it could not see
is worse.

I measured it two further ways before believing either:

1. **Git provenance** (independent of pixels): for every shipped cell with a paired master,
   is the shipped cell's last-touching commit newer than its master's? → **26 cells: 17
   `char-baron-sheet-walk8`, 9 `char-hero-sheet-walk8`.** This method **predicted baron=17
   before any pixel was compared.**
2. **The pixel control itself**, run by me on main:
   - `--verify-downscale char-hero-sheet-walk8` → `23 byte-identical, 9 mismatched` — the
     runner's numbers **and filenames**, exactly.
   - `--verify-downscale char-baron-sheet-walk8` → `15 byte-identical, **17 mismatched**` —
     confirming the provenance prediction exactly.
   - **Whole tree, no stem filter** → `389 byte-identical, **26 mismatched**, 0 with no
     shipped copy (of 415 masters)`. 9 + 17 = 26. **No third sheet exists.** The class is
     closed and complete.

**Provenance accounts for all 26, and the runner's account of its 9 is exact:**
`21dc8739` *"fix-walk-cutout-pockets"* (2026-07-13) changed **80** shipped cells and **zero**
masters; `04732223` *"town-cast-metrology"* (2026-07-12) changed **3** shipped cells and
**zero** masters. Of those, the ones with a paired master are precisely our 26 (7 hero + 17
baron from the first; 2 hero from the second).

**Why it matters, stated at its real severity — LATENT, not live.** `optimize-assets.mjs`'s
flagless main loop reads `assets/processed-full/`, downscales, and **overwrites the shipped
cell**, for every file, every run (`maybeRefreshFullCopy` only writes a master when one is
*missing*). So a plain global run silently **reverts** the cutout-pocket and metrology fixes
on 26 cells — across the hero (what a player watches all run) and the baron. ✓ I verified
this is **armed but not automatic**: `optimize-assets.mjs` has **no automated caller** — not
in `package.json`, not in any script or CI. It is a manual step, and `anim-pass-reextract.mjs`
exists precisely to avoid it. So nothing is broken in today's build; a loaded gun is lying on
the bench with a handwritten sign on it.

### F-1176-2 — THE CONTROL THAT STOPPED THE RUN CANNOT EVER PASS ON THESE TWO SHEETS, AND WILL BLOCK THE NEXT RUNG TOO.

`--verify-downscale` (`anim-pass-reextract.mjs:102-118`) re-derives shipped cells from their
masters and byte-compares, to prove *this script's resize matches `optimize-assets.mjs`'s*.
That claim is **true and now strongly evidenced** — 389 of 415 cells reproduce byte-identically.

The problem is its **reference set**, not its arithmetic. Its own comment says it verifies
against *"untouched shipped cells"* — but **"untouched" is an assumption the code never checks
and cannot check.** There is no exclusion list, no provenance test; it iterates every master
and ends `process.exit(bad ? 1 : 0)`. So **any sheet containing even one deliberate
post-extraction mend is permanently a red for this control**, for a reason that has nothing
to do with the claim being verified.

➡️ **Consequence for the ladder, and this is the actionable part:** the baron is one of the
five non-hero walk8 slots the next EIGHT-WINDS rung must bind. It carries **17** contaminated
cells — nearly double the hero's. **The next rung will hit this same wall, harder**, and so
will every future animation mend on either sheet. Repairing this is now on the critical path
of the whole EIGHT-WINDS ladder, which is why the corrective is authored rather than filed.

### F-1176-4 — the goal vocabulary has no word for "deferred behind a successor", so the honest status gives the right ACTION with a misleading REASON. (Extends F-1174-1.)

This rung needed a status meaning *"do not queue now; re-queue this exact master unchanged once
its blocker lands."* No such word exists. The available arms (`drain-block-check.mjs:46`,
`:199-218`):

- **`blocked`** → §3.0 defines a block as lifted *by the OWNER only*. Using it would manufacture
  an owner gate where none exists — precisely the error s1172 and s1174 each declined to make.
- **`stopped`** (chosen) → correctly refuses the queue arm (verified: rc=1), but prints
  *"This master's question is dead — typically overturned or carried down by a successor that
  merged … author a SUCCESSOR; do not revive this leaf."* **The question is not dead — it is
  deferred**, and slice 1's master is correct as written (all three of its scope-1 premises were
  confirmed in the lane). A fire obeying that text literally would re-author a good master from
  scratch.

I chose the arm with the right **action** over the one with the right-sounding reason, and then
closed the gap in writing rather than leaving it to be re-derived: the leaf's `stopNote` and a
loud ⚠️ block in the successor task both instruct the draining fire to flip the leaf back to
`queued` and re-queue the existing file. **Recommendation** (cheap, but a shared vocabulary, so
attended not drive-by): a `deferred` arm carrying a `deferredBehind: <leaf-id>` pointer, refusing
the queue arm while its blocker is unshipped and clearing automatically once that blocker merges.
Note this needs no owner *decision* — unlike F-1174-1 it is a pure mechanism question.
