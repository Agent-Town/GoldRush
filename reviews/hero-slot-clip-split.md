**Verdict:** MERGED. Owner's words, verbatim: "now it loads veerrry slowly"; this morning "yes, lets do 1". Measured first and reproduced exactly: the hero's runtime slot is 8,901,114 B in 141 responses inside the first-town window on both projects; `char-hero-sheet-work8` (29 cells, 3,552,007 B) and `attack8` (6 cells, 758,822 B) are the claim's clips, and the town plays neither (`TownScene.ts:743` calls `hero.update()` with no `panning` argument, so `Hero.ts:129` defaults it false, and `playAttackPose`'s only caller is `Game.ts:594`). The design: a clip carries a group in the character contract (`clipGroups` per slot: a `default` plus per-clip overrides); the slot's default group always loads and carries `walk`/`idle`, the fallback chain `pickClip` already degrades to, so a clip whose group has not arrived plays the fallback and cannot throw; non-default groups load when a scene declares them (`SPRITE_CLIP_GROUPS`: the town declares `['town']`, the claim `['town','claim']`, each at module scope, strictly before that scene's own `new Hero()`) and otherwise warm on the advance stream's idle callback when its plan drains (never on `allowance`, which means a metered connection spent its budget); groups merge into the live `RuntimeSlot`. Result in the deploy's instrument, identical on both projects in every run: the window 20,412,264 → 16,101,435 (desktop) / 16,052,745 (mobile) B; the hero slot 8,901,114 / 141 → 4,590,285 / 106; `work8` + `attack8` 35 responses → 0; other PNG families unchanged. The swing stall measured 0 ms in all twelve cells (two arms × cold/warm claim × three runs): on a cold claim entry the attack cells complete before the hero sprite is playable at all.

**The honest negatives, recorded rather than smoothed:** warming on the first idle tick after `assetLoadingState=ready` moved nothing (the probe observes that signal by polling, and 4.3 MB fits inside its skirt), so the warm fires when the plan drains; the wall clock does not show the saving on this host (paired town-ready 2,126 → 2,501 ms loopback, 5,838 → 5,780 ms at 8 Mbps, with two runs of the same build 25 % apart under four concurrent implementers), so no town-ready improvement is claimed; the payload is the reproducible fact. Stated limit in the code: if the advance stream is never armed (`terrain2d` / lite tier) the plan drains on tick one and the warm never fires; the claim group then arrives on the claim scene's own declaration.
