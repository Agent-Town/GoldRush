## What it does
- **State:** `assay: 'retired'` as a fifth terminal value (chosen over an additive marker because every reader that narrows the union lives in `standings.ts`, except `src/encyclopedia/reader.ts:695`, which only ever sees ranked rows and a retired row is never ranked; asserted in the fixture). Transitions: `verified` → (reassay verb) → `pending` + `lineage { reason, requeuedAt }` with score, party, stack and `submittedAt` untouched so the board does not flicker; the worker's `verified` → `verified` again with the same `submittedAt` (the first-secure date) and a new `assayedAt`; the worker's `rejected` → `retired` with `assayReason = lineage: recorded under <tape engineHash>, no longer replays under <current engineHash>` (`standings.ts:402`); `unassayable` stays `unassayable` with the mark kept (an instrument failure is not evidence). Retired rows are unranked (`:1081`), counted in `retiredCount` beside the era-5 refusals (`:420`), still served by `?reel=` and `?verdict=` (which now also serves the `lineage` cause).
- **Verb:** `POST /api/standings/reassay { epochId, contractId, reason }` on the assayer's secret path (`standings.ts:370`; the Pages route shim `functions/api/standings/reassay.ts`, F-LINEAGE-7): 503 without a secret, 401 wrong key, 405 non-POST, 400 `bad_reassay` on an unknown contract, a missing or over-long reason, or any extra key; rate-limit exempt (the limiter lives in `submitScore` alone); idempotent (only `verified` rows flip; a second call returns `requeued: 0` and writes nothing). The worker's wire vocabulary is unchanged, so door and droplet need no lockstep deploy.
- **Trigger:** `scripts/assay-lineage-sweep.mjs`: for each door contract the latest engine pin whose `cause` names it; the door's verified rows and each reel's recorded pin ordinal; any row recorded before the naming pin makes the contract a candidate. Dry by default; `--commit` needs `ASSAY_WORKER_SECRET`. Over-inclusive on purpose: the replay decides, never the pattern match. NOT wired into `scripts/deploy.sh` (F-LINEAGE-5: a network leg in the publish path with a credential not provably present there); the one-line hook is in `docs/assay-worker-runbook.md`.

## Findings
- **F-LINEAGE-1 (cured in-slice, would have deleted standings):** a verified row's score is its recorded `securedSnapshot`, which may differ from its reel's outcome; flipping it to `pending` put it back under the `tapeMatchesScore` arm and it would have stopped validating and vanished on the next read. Cured at `standings.ts:1028-1030`.
- **F-LINEAGE-2:** `src/encyclopedia/reader.ts:695` cannot survive a new assay state; any future slice that ranks a retired row must widen that reader first.
- **F-LINEAGE-3 (cured by the drain):** `public/skill.md:42` told riders the slip answers `verified` or `rejected`.
- **F-LINEAGE-4 (non-blocking):** `server/ledger/serve.mjs:21-33` routes the queue and verdict paths but not the verb; the droplet ledger does not serve it; the live door is Pages, so harmless today. One row.
- **F-LINEAGE-6 (cured by the drain):** the generator consulted the stored receipt only in the claimed branch, so a contract whose LAST verified row retires would be rewritten `unclaimed`, erasing its first-secure fact: F-RECEIPTS-1 by a new route.
- **F-LINEAGE-8 (pre-existing, fire-authorable):** 14 of 62 standings e2e are red because their fixture tapes carry no `meta.engineHash`/`meta.era` and the era-5 submission law refuses them (`field-book`, `mp-07c-4-reckoning`, one each in `milk-county-board` / `assay-season-roll` / `lb-01-county-standings`); the era-5 drain never updated the fixtures.
