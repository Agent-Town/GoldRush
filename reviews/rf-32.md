## What it does

`setupProfileStorage()` (`StartMenu.ts:419`) degraded cleanly under mode-1 blocked storage only **by accident**: the first thing that threw was an unguarded `storage.getItem` buried inside `hasLegacyProfileData`, and that throw — caught by the enclosing `:426` catch — is what made the function return `undefined`, which is what kept `firstBoot` false and the "Who's prospecting?" screen off the page.

s1093 measured that F-1084-2's own prescription (re-point that `getItem` at the `rawGet` accessor rf-22 exported) would have **removed the only thing making the degrade work**. This slice therefore does it in the safe order:

1. adds an **explicit** mode-1 usability probe (`storage.getItem(RUN_SUSPEND_KEY)`) directly after the `:421` null check, with a comment naming what it is and warning that `rawGet` must never be substituted (it swallows, so it cannot signal unusability);
2. **then** re-points `hasLegacyProfileData` at `rawGet`;
3. re-points `migrateLegacySuspendResources` at `rawGet`/`rawSet` **while keeping its `try`/`catch`** — that catch also guards the `JSON.parse`, which `rawGet` cannot cover;
4. pins the contract with a **plain-boot** oracle (`profile-first-boot.spec.ts:258`) asserting `profile-title` has count **0** under mode-1 blocked storage, plus an explicit `Illegal invocation` guard.

Two files, **+46 / −3**. `rawGet`/`rawSet` gain their first consumers outside `ProfileStorage.ts` — rf-22's export was dead capability until now.
