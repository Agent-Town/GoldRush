## What it does

On a greenhorn's first entry to town after naming, the newsie delivers the Gazette in person and then walks the player through three anchored beats — **Tavern board → works stake → Schoolhouse chart** — each skippable, dissolving back into normal play. A profile-scoped once-flag (`TOWN_WELCOME_SEEN_KEY`, additive to `ProfileStorage`) makes it fire exactly once and never replay uninvited; the newsie carries a retrigger prompt so it can be asked for again on purpose. New module `src/town/TownWelcome.ts` holds the phase machine (`idle`/`paper`/`walk`, arrival-radius beat advance); `TownScene.ts` holds the hook.
