## What it does
Owner bug (verbatim master WHY, 2026-07-09): internal authoring facts were leaking to the player through the Claim Ledger — loreLines carried doc citations like `(lore/README.md places law; docs/GOLD_RUSH_BRIEF.md §2)`, and fact lines exposed roster/batch/OWNER-RULING scaffolding. 063 replaces the leak with an in-world voice:
- **`src/encyclopedia/reader.ts`** — new `playerLedgerLine()` sanitizer strips trailing `(… lore/ | docs/ | *.md | YYYY-MM-DD …)` citation parentheticals and drops any line matching `INTERNAL_LEDGER_PATTERNS` (lore/, .md, batch-, dates, "OWNER RULING", "NEVER", roster, wiki, backend, docs/, codex-, and internal stat prefixes like `Ability:`/`Move speed:`). Every discovered card now prefers a hand-authored **character QUOTE** (`data-testid="claim-ledger-character-quote"`), falling back to a sanitized loreLine only when no quote exists. `renderFacts()` extracted; render-only.
- **`src/encyclopedia/registry.ts`** — the leaky loreLines rewritten to clean in-world copy ("The river stake is where the town begins.", "The Baron rides under an oxblood banner.", etc.); no citation tails.
- **`lore/characters.md`** — adds `QUOTE:` lines per character (hero, prospector, baron, claim_jumper, elder, tavernkeeper, clerk, preacher, storekeeper, schoolteacher, youngsters) — canon source for the in-game quotes. Read-before-write honored; additive.
- **`src/encyclopedia/reader.css`** — `.claim-ledger-card__quote` style.
- **e2e `en-01` + `en-02`** — assertions extended for the quote element and the no-internal-leak guarantee.

Render-only UI/copy change. **Firewall respected:** touches encyclopedia reader + registry + lore + en specs only. No sim, no gameplay logic, no economy, no combat.
