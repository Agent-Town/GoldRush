## What it does

After a suspend/restore at a site where terrain changed, the Prospector came back at the wrong
visual height. The runner's diagnosis instrumented the whole round trip and named the exact
divergence: captured `0.14559222393281415` → normalized `0.14559222393281415` → restored
`0.14559222393281415` → **terrain re-sync `0.2763519114255905`**. `restoreHero` had copied Y
exactly, and then `game.syncHeroVisualHeight?.()` — one line later — resampled it off the new terrain.

Two one-line fixes:

- **`src/game/RunSuspend.ts:843`** — after the terrain sync, re-apply the snapshot's hero Y.
  This is **option (A)** of the F-1096-2 fork, but scoped *narrower than the July deletion*
  (`f1fce725`, which removed the sync call outright): other actors keep terrain resampling,
  only the hero regains its saved render height.
- **`src/game/Game.ts:3456`** — `restoreAgentRiderSnapshots` accepts an absent `agentRiders`
  field when there are zero rider bodies. Strict normalization drops the optional multiplayer
  extension, so single-player snapshots were failing a validation for riders that cannot exist.
  Validation is unchanged when riders *do* exist.

None of the master's **four forbidden greens** was taken: no `e2e/` edit, no exclusion of
`hero.position` from the comparison, no rounding/epsilon, no copying post-restore Y back into
the snapshot. The diff is 2 added lines in the 2 permitted source files.
