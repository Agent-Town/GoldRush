## Findings
- **F-HMV-1 (design, OWNER'S DESK):** an active `MOVE_HERO` owns the executor's single tick, so a rider cannot walk the hero and work the Prospector in the same tick; the Prospector coasts and drifts to the hero meanwhile. That is the structural reason kiting costs kills. A parallel hero channel (two order arrays, or a per-body executor) is a design decision.
- **F-HMV-2 (hygiene, cured):** the preserve spec's screenshot helper rewrites tracked evidence under `artifacts/e10s-3-preserve/shots` and `artifacts/e10s-4-door/shots` on every run; restored.
- **F-HMV-3 (pre-existing, fire-authorable):** `agent-view.spec.ts:639`'s seeded snapshot is stale on `viewVersion` and `coalSeams`.
- **F-HMV-4 (measurement):** kiting is not a winning tactic on the maps that motivated the verb; riders now have the choice a human has, and the notebooks will tell whether any map rewards it.
