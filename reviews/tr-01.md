## What it does
Adds a continuous, indexed ground mesh (`src/world/ContinuousGroundMesh.ts`) displaced by the render-side VISUAL height, replacing the tile-quad ground patchwork **only when the new `terrainMesh` flag is ON** (Balance knob `world.terrainMesh` + `VITE_GR_TERRAIN_MESH` env, DEFAULT OFF). Same per-region bank textures apply via per-region UVs, so this slice changes GEOMETRY only — lighting/normals become continuous (edge shading softens) while texture seams remain by design until TR-02's splat. The ground renders in a single draw call in both flag states. Sim/TileHeight untouched (planar sim preserved). Diagnostics surface through `DebugTools` for the e2e probes.
