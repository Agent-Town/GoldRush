## What it does
Makes the 3D town read its era. The per-building pilot loader now prefers `<building>.e<N>.glb` for the active epoch and falls back down the chain (e3→e2→base), data-driven with zero per-building code — it works today with zero era variants present (everything falls back to base) and lights up automatically as Sol's variants land. When a mounted variant carries `steam_anchor_*` nodes and the active era is E2+, the existing plume/steam emitters mount at those anchors from one shared particle pool (LITE tier mounts no plumes). Town-wide, an era accent (`data-town-era-accent` 1→2) dresses the plaza for the new age from existing assets. Owner directive verbatim (2026-07-14): *"I would have expected all the houses using some form of steam and the change to E2 showing in town as well… The player should [k]now that a new time is starting - also visually."*
