
## A Rush That Killed You Still Counts What You Banked

Secure a claim, then stay for the Rush and die, and the office ledger used to print nothing at all in the earnings column — as though the run had come to nothing.
The claim was banked the moment you secured it, and the page now says so: the Territory, Science, Hero and Agent you rode home with are printed whether the Rush spared you or not.
Runs that ended before a claim was ever secured still print no earnings line, exactly as before.
