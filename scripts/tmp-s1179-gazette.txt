

## She Was Drawn Walking The Diagonals A Fortnight Ago. Today She Wears Them
- The figure you play had eight ways to walk and four sets of drawings. Head north-east and you were shown her plain east art, turned loose on a heading it was never painted for; the same borrowing on all four corners.
- Thirty-two new drawings — eight strides for each of the four diagonals — are now hers, and they keep step with the four she already had, so a turn from north to north-east no longer changes her stride.
- The art had been finished and sitting in the store room since the eight-winds shift; nothing about her walking was rebuilt to hang it, only the wardrobe list she reads from.
