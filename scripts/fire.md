## 1. Lock protocol
1. If STATUS.md line-1 starts with "ACTIVE" and the stamp is <45 min old: another fire is live — EXIT silently.
2. Otherwise: rewrite line-1 to `ACTIVE <ISO-stamp> (s<N> fire) — <one-line intent>`, commit it (`s<N>: lock ACTIVE — <intent>`), and proceed. A >45-min-stale ACTIVE lock is dead: archive it honestly in your handoff and take over.
3. **STAMPS COME FROM A COMMAND, NEVER FROM ARITHMETIC (F-1039-2, closed s1053).** Derive every stamp by running `date "+%Y-%m-%dT%H:%MZ"` and pasting the result. Hand-computing keeps drifting stamps into the **future** — s1038 **+46 min**, s1046 **+56/+50 min**, and s1053's own mid-fire refresh **+14 min**, written by a fire that had just read this very finding. That is not cosmetic: §1.1 tells the next fire to **EXIT SILENTLY** while an `ACTIVE` lock is <45 min old, so a future-dated stamp left by a fire that then dies **stalls the entire factory for the skew plus 45 minutes**, and the staleness law's own clock is the thing that is wrong. ⚠️ **AND THE STAMP TEXT IS NOT TRUSTWORTHY, SO DO NOT JUDGE STALENESS BY IT.** Measured s1053: the trailing `Z` is a **mislabel** — Robin's Mac is Bangkok (**UTC+07**), so a `date`-derived stamp reads `02:21Z` when true UTC is `19:21Z`. **The convention is genuinely INCONSISTENT across fires, which is the real hazard:** s1050/s1051/s1052 all stamped **local** (s1050's own text says "it is 00:56 local" against a `00:56+07` commit), while s1047–s1049 were recorded as switching to **true UTC** (`date -u`). Two conventions, ~7 h apart, both labeled `Z`, plus hand-computed errors of **+14 to +56 min**. ➡️ **THEREFORE THE BINDING RULE FOR §1.1 IS: judge a lock's age by its OWNING COMMIT, not by the stamp text** — `git log -1 --format=%cI` on the commit that wrote line-1. That is timezone-proof and arithmetic-proof. A ~7 h "gap" is far more likely a UTC-vs-local stamp than a dead fire; **never take over a lock on stamp text alone.** Write new stamps with plain `date "+%Y-%m-%dT%H:%MZ"` (local, matching the three most recent fires) for continuity, and fix the label only as a deliberate flag-day landing in every fire at once — **never as a drive-by.**
