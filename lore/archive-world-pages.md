## Wing 1, west stacks — "The Catalog of Answered Doors"
A shipping ledger from the old world's catalog civilization (the warehouse E4 found; the sea-floor prototypes E5 found): page after page of orders FILLED — and the last column, delivery confirmations, goes quiet mid-page. No catastrophe recorded. The orders simply stop being acknowledged. The final entry is stamped with the old world's own pictogram for "answer pending."
(Canon: the wider world is old, `STORYBOOK.md:336`; the world stopped answering, `:418`.)

## Wing 2, east stacks — "The Minutes of the Last Standards Body"
A committee's minutes, decades apart: first arguing gauge widths and signal codes with joyful pedantry; then arguing attendance; then a single secretary recording that no delegates came, moving that the standard be preserved as written, seconding her own motion, adjourning. The page is initialed twice by the same hand.
(Tone law: warm — no wreckage described; absence carries it.)

## Wing 3, warning shelf — "The Instruction That Was Followed"
The old world's own recall notice — their "COME HOME," issued generations before the town's (`STORYBOOK.md:418`) — with distribution marks showing it was received everywhere, and a homecoming registry that was never filled in. Beneath it, in a different, later hand, the pictogram sequence the town reads as: *kept for whoever asks next.*
This page is the map's thesis and the era's warning, shown never said — and the "ours, unless." shelf (`e10-archive-world` tileParams, `emptyShelfZone`) re-inks its frame only when all three wings stand restored. The question it asks is the point.
