
## The Prospector Takes Panning Orders One Rung Sooner

Standing orders to work a claim used to wait on the third rung of trust, kept back alongside the raising of buildings.
Panning answers at the second rung now — working a claim is the hero's plain daily labour, not a privilege held back for the far end of the ladder.
Raising a building still waits for the third, and rightly so: timber and nails come out of the strongbox, and that is a different kind of trust.
