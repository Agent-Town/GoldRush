

## GAZETTE — The County Lends You A Corner Of The River Claim, And Keeps No Record Of It

There is a sixth job on the Frontier board now: the Drill Yard, a borrowed corner of the river claim at the edge of town, where the county lends a hundred gold and asks for none of it back.
Straw men and rolling logs take a hit, fall over and stand back up; every Frontier building is on offer whether or not you have researched it; and a bell rings one small wave of eight when you want one.
Nothing in the yard enters the county ledger — no score, no standing, no run history, no tape. You can walk in, try the whole toolbox on a straw man, walk out, and the books will not show you were ever there.
The card is on the board from the first boot, before any welcome and with no unlock, which is the point of a place to practise.
