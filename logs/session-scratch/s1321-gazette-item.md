
## GAZETTE — The Ground Stopped Re-Reading Its Own Seed Thirty Times A Sample

Every patch of ground the works draw is derived from the map seed, and the ground was fetching that seed out of the address bar and re-reading it from scratch each time it asked — about twenty-eight times for a single height lookup, and the hero asks for one of those every frame.
It now reads the seed once and remembers it until the seed itself changes, which is the whole fix: four lines, nothing different on screen, and the map comes out identical down to the last stone.
This also settles the bill the last roundup flagged: the screenless practice runner that had slowed to nearly nine times its old cost is back down, with Night Shift falling from about twenty seconds to under seven.
