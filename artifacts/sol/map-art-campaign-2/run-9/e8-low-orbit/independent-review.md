# Low Orbit — independent final visual critique

A fresh screenshot-critique agent inspected the concept plate, paired ordinary desktop/phone captures, and labelled 3x inspection crops. It reviewed the revised final candidate after identifying weak teal accents and ambiguous surfaces in the first candidate.

Final findings, all high confidence:

- Panel seams make the conical caps read as assembled pieces. The larger teal console and brighter tips restore useful accents. The bolted rim, deep opening and supported drum are substantially clearer than the original crossing hoops.
- The deck no longer resembles streaky timber, but its uniform beige surface looks unfinished beside the textured machine. Contact shading beneath the body, feet and character remains weak. No definite floating-geometry error was found.
- Both side appendages are more visible. Their straight segments and blunt tips still read as outriggers or braces, short of the reference's curved opposing pincers and conspicuous articulated joints.
- Ordinary phone cards hide the gantry and much of the rear-left tower. Inspection improves framing, but its tooltip still covers the upper-left machine and part of the opening. Neither an unobstructed desktop crop nor a low persistent-HUD mask proves ordinary phone readability.
- The cavity, pointed towers and brass/teal palette correspond to the plate. Radiating bridges, peripheral rings, hanging structures and dense mechanical detail remain absent; the rectangular gantry emphasizes a frontier well silhouette more than orbital machinery.
