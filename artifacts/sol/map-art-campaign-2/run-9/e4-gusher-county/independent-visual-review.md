# Independent visual review — Gusher County

Fresh agent e4_gusher_review inspected the concept, before/after derricks 01 and 02, phone 01, and final 04/08 station views. Initial verdict: after is clearly less wrong through tapered towers, cross-bracing, crown platforms and footings, but the pipe/winch assembly remained too weak.

A focused revision widened the local pipe, added bolted couplings and an exposed spoked drive wheel. The reviewer re-opened the revised 01 desktop/phone and 02 desktop images. The wheel is clearly visible at both widths; the dark front run and brass collars now read more convincingly as pipe. The tower plus wheel, gauge, valve and fittings is a substantial local improvement, with no obvious silhouette/contact regression.

Retained limitations: mechanism remains schematic; wheel-to-winch connection is unclear; side elbow stays dark/partly obscured; uniform timber and sparse metal weathering; modest variant richness. Skids/feet make contact plausible but the dark ground and soft shadow prevent precise visual proof. Steep cameras hide side detail; dialogue/UI reduce scene context. No broader fluid network or visible gusher spectacle is established. Full field concept acceptance remains withheld.
