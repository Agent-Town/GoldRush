# Archive World — rejected candidates and evidence boundary

The first nine box-like library halls were too tall/dark and clipped at entry. A lower masonry version still read as a box, so it was superseded by four colonnaded ruins with actual arched facade openings and recessed walls. No previous candidate is credited as final evidence. Sources and partial captures remain in local ignored raw evidence.

The first full 44-case browser batch was explicitly terminated at case 8 while revising the scenery. Its exit 143 is an interrupted attempt, not an accepted pass/failure result. The final batch uses the final retained source. The first saved-source replay also used an erroneous `archive-world-shore` filename; it failed and was corrected. Blender uses `--python-exit-code 1`; the final byte-equivalent replay receipt is the accepted result.

Restoration state tests are labelled staged paint only. They never establish native objective completion. Ordinary entry/HUD and the original five landmark body geometries remain unchanged. The new scenery lies outside the playable square and adds no collision or walk surface.
