# River — independent visual review

A fresh reviewer inspected ordinary desktop/phone before/after captures, the concept, and 2x bank/ford crops. The first complete candidate was rejected for conspicuous large angular additions. The final candidate was re-inspected independently.

Final findings, high confidence:
- Smaller additions blend with the existing clusters; the earlier consistency risk is materially reduced. Angular low-detail stones remain noticeable in crops but no longer dominate the full view.
- Bank coverage and crossing legibility improve; scale variety is better and geometric clutter is reduced.
- Ford stones still describe two parallel tracks around a broad empty center. Irregular rocky shallows remain incomplete.
- Water lacks local stone ripples/wakes; diffuse margins and broad olive bands remain.
- Banks remain sparse between isolated stones and clusters; terrain remains uniformly mottled.
- Mobile upper HUD hides crossing/far-bank detail; dialogue obscures Claim Stake and controls. Desktop dialogue also overlaps Claim Stake.
- No obvious new seams, clipping, or severe rendering artifacts in the inspected stills.

Primary-agent disposition: accept the bounded density, scale and water-color improvement; retain full gravel continuity, bank relief, localized water contact and concept fidelity as art-owned holds. Preserve camera/UI holds with their owners. Ground-intersection proof establishes embedded geometry, not contact-shadow quality.
