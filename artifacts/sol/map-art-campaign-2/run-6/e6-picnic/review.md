# The Picnic — run 6 correction

**IMPROVED / HELD.** Three authored picnic blankets, their tableware/baskets and the passable staging gate now mount alongside the original collision-backed bodies. The ground is quieter and the legacy whole-body emission exemption is removed. The ordinary phone entry still lacks the picnic grouping; full concept acceptance remains withheld.

The underlying issue was more than paint: the Picnic pack existed, but the runtime's Glow Mesa alias never selected it. `src/world/LandmarkCollision.ts` also aliases Picnic to Glow Mesa. Replacing all five base bodies would leave their active blockers invisible; mounting the solid shade at (0,40) would show an obstacle without its declared blocker. This correction retains every base body, uses the existing mount/filter mechanism for four authored nonblocking bodies, and explicitly defers the shade in both mirrored contracts. No mount transform or collision meaning changes.
