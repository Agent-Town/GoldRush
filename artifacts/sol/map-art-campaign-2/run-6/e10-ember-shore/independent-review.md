## What holds up

The altar's round cap, framed windows, cylinder, and square footing form a coherent, recognizable piece of brass machinery related to the concept. The titan's two articulated arms remain distinguishable at full size, with no obvious attachment break. Ground planes and prop bases generally agree in perspective. The player remains easy to locate in the station and southeast frames. I do not see a definite hard texture seam in `crop-join.png`; the concern there is patterned, bland ground rather than a proven join defect. Enlarged edges are soft/pixelated as expected, but gross blur is not the main full-size problem.

The strongest remaining art issues are titan identity/materials and terrain character. Phone overlay collisions are independently visible and should be tracked even if they predate this art pass.

## Final-candidate reassessment — 2026-09-22 (Asia/Bangkok)

Reopened the replacement station, desktop, phone, and southeast full frames in that order, then the replacement `crop-titan.png` and `crop-ground.png`. The observations below supersede material/terrain statements above where the candidate visibly changed. This is a local presentation reassessment, not full-concept acceptance.

The surface still lacks a strong metallic light response, but no conspicuous stretched grain remains in this crop. | High; full station frame and `crop-titan.png`. |
