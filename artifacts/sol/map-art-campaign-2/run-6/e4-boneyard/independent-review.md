# Independent visual review — Boneyard

Fresh agent, screenshots only, no implementation history. The reviewer found improved ground readability and a recognizable engine at the inspection station, but withheld full acceptance. High-confidence findings: stretched ground marks and upper boundary persist; plain entry lacks graveyard identity; barrel streaks read as wood; bright linkage dominates dark chassis; insufficient burial/contact; angular rear support clips the phone weapon HUD. Plate density and mechanical articulation remain richer.

Resolution in progress: improve burial and the barrel UV projection, retain the other gaps as explicit art/layout/UI holds.

## Resolution

A second independent check confirmed the cylindrical UV projection reads closer to corrosion, and partial wheel coverage increases the sunken impression, but rejected the first rubble group as rectangular paving-like blocks. Those eight separate pieces were replaced by one irregular, tapered soil deposit. Direct inspection of the final station confirms the block silhouettes are gone. The final deposit remains subtle: extensive burial, richer worn metal and yard-wide density are still art holds, not declared fixed. The rear support retains a small phone HUD overlap, measured in the final masks. No full concept acceptance is claimed.
