## Architecture and ground

The concept is organized as a complete circular brass platform: radial floor divisions, concentric engraved bands, perimeter railings, gates, and distinct monuments around a relatively open center. The gameplay images instead show a close view of gray cobblestones crossed by broad pale arcs, with isolated pale monuments. The arcs and lantern establish some common geometry, but the visible materials, lighting, and floor construction do not strongly convey the concept's machinery or brass platform. This is a high-confidence difference, not an assumption that every concept feature must be reproduced.

The screenshots do not expose enough of the arena boundary to judge whether the gates, railings, outer platform, or radial layout exist elsewhere. The portrait is only partially visible at the right edge of the desktop shot, so its complete shape cannot be assessed.

The paving and curved bands appear mutually consistent in perspective. The lantern roof, body, and stepped base appear attached; the base meets the floor plausibly. I do not see a definite floating lantern, detached roof, or incorrect front/back overlap in the supplied crops. Its roughly monument-sized scale is not intrinsically a defect. The main depth/readability concern is effect and label occlusion around the neighboring preserve.

The grayscale scene separates poorly into lit focal objects, secondary props, and background paving, while the bright cream UI remains very prominent. Overall, the player/lantern relationship is legible, but the three-preserve composition and the concept's warm architectural identity are not clearly communicated in these views.
