# Independent visual review

Fresh agent inspected only the desktop/phone boards and 2x river crops, without implementation history. Verdict: candidate is less wrong, high confidence, as a bounded water/readability improvement; full concept fidelity remains unaccepted.

- Fine horizontal streaks improve directional flow cues over the old blurred chevrons.
- Olive water, parallel banks and dark patches still read as a rectangular band; thin flow lines can look mechanically uniform.
- Reduced dark ground blotches separate characters and their shadows more clearly, although the concept has richer terrain detail.
- The entry still does not communicate terraced beds, repeated steaming machinery or rocky riverbank construction.
- Dialogue overlaps claim information and crowds phone controls. No obvious new prop-ordering or attachment defect was visible. Stills do not establish animation quality.

These remaining findings are retained in the bounded verdict; this review is not owner acceptance.
