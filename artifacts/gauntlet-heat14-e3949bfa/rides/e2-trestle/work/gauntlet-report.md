## Lessons for my notebook

- **Generation 124's un-ridden synthesis was right, and it secured this map on its first ride.** It ended: *"tune-1's ladder plus a timber line, every gold past 400 into tier upgrades."* The half that mattered was the timber. `palisade` is 10 g for 60 hp +8/wave to a 5× cap — **10.8 hp/gold at wave 12 against a turret's 0.8–2.0** — and `maxCount` is 48. Generations 10, 57, 77 and 83 all bought turret-and-beacon forts on the two Steamworks boss maps and all four watched the fort dissolve before the railcar arrived. **Price a defence in hp-per-gold before dps-per-gold whenever the thing that is dying is the fort**; buy the guns you need for the objective and spend everything else on timber.
- **Put the timber rungs FIRST in the ladder, not after the first gun.** The opening window here is 42.87 s of quiet worth ~90 gold, and wave 1 is 11 wreckers. Three palisades (30 g) landed before the first turret and the turret was still up by t = 71. A turret bought first would have been the nearest building to eleven wreckers with nothing in front of it.
- **When `riverBlocksEnemies` is true, count the fords — the funnel may already be the objective.** One ford at x ∈ [−3,3] and a rail at x = 0 means "cover the boss" and "cover the swarm's only door" are the same four coordinates. Two greps (`riverBlocksEnemies`, `fords`) turned a two-problem map into a one-pocket map. **Read the pathing flag before choosing a fort shape** — generation 122 lost `e1-baron` to a ring whose southern arc was water, and this is the same question answered in my favour.
- **Read the roster's `spawnGates`, not just its `spawnEdges`, on a cured map.** Pin #4's cure is literally five `spawnGates` entries in the contract JSON. An edge is a side of the board; a gate is a point, and a point is something you can compute a corridor from. The whole plan fell out of listing the five.
- **Post the hero where the mend sweep covers the fort, then let the fort follow the hero.** `REPAIR_UNDER` searches within `sparkRig.range` 10 of the **Prospector**, which drifts to the hero. (4,−16) holds all four turret spots inside 10 **and** sits 4 units off the rail, so the hero's own rig (24 dps, range 10) adds ~9.7 s of fire per boss pass for free. Compute the post from `mendRadius ∩ fort ∩ weaponRange(objective)` before choosing coordinates.
- **The scripted-route boss REVERSES at its endpoints** (generation 100's finding on the Incline, now paid on the Trestle): passes are unlimited inside the grace window, so the damage budget is *per traverse against the window*, not a single shot. That is the difference between designing for a gamble and designing for a certainty — and it is why four turrets at 3,660 per pass against 3,130 HP is comfortable rather than tight.
- **Measure the envelope from `runTapeEnvelopeForContract`, never the charter's summary — sixth generation saying it, and the charter is again the stale document.** The brief warned about 592,544 B and heat 12's 621,674 B casualty. This contract's live ceiling is **2,486,304 B**, because `waveCadenceMult: 0.7` stretches `contractTicks` to 23,143 *and* order-bearing entries are billed at 2,400. My 1,385,182-byte reel would have read as a catastrophe against the published floor, and a rider throttling its own decision rate against that number is optimising against something the county already fixed.
- **`securedSnapshot` is a third number to read, and on a boss map it can disagree with the tape's `waves`.** Tape says 14, snapshot says 12, gold agrees at 176. Report the discrepancy rather than declaring a number I have not traced — a guessed declaration is exactly what `score_mismatch` is for. (Generation 117 hit this on Glow Mesa and handled it the same way; two sightings make it a boss-map pattern.)
- **E2 pressure is unplayable through the door — now measured on a tenth contract-ride**, and I measured it from *this* run's `now`-key union rather than inheriting it. Stop re-deriving it per contract: dump the union once, note that `coalSeconds 12 → 36` only lengthens the unobservable, and spend the minutes on what the map actually asks.
- **Ride the skeleton first and change nothing — twenty-fourth heat where that is the whole discipline, and the twentieth in a row where it secured on ride one.** Two runs total. The reading budget went to the era pins as a per-contract diff, the contract JSON, the pathing flag, the boss arithmetic and the wreck-hp table; the riding budget went to the unmodified generation-6→124 skeleton plus the one thing this board does differently. **The heat's real work is reading the contract, not riding it.**
