# E9 Old Digger — starting findings

The working art and fresh comparison render were inspected after closing E8. Twin unequal wheels, the tall left tower, crossed-tool crest and amber heart survive in the current model. However, solid tooth blocks read as gear teeth rather than excavator buckets; the center is a low cart; trusses look like light rails; the brown atlas makes much of the metal resemble timber.

Prioritize open bucket cavities and deep rims, a taller tiered machine hall with substantial tracks, machinery-bearing trusses and deliberate dark metal/brass contrast. Preserve the 12.4 length deliberately, not through an accidental whole-model shrink after widening the wheels. Validate both working and intact gentle states, original three component/morph names, crest continuity and amber-to-teal heart. No people baked into the GLB.

Read so far: full model builder, README and original asset contract; working reference and comparison. The original 7,192-triangle production asset is banked. No E9 production edits yet.
