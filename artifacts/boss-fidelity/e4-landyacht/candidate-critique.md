# Land Yacht candidate — visible issues to resolve

Fresh independent screenshot critique, 2026-09-08, against candidate GLB `79eefec72fb8d726ab950015f6e5e395f4aacdf4f645afdad3ea5ff45b3b31a2`. Full concept plates, current neutral images and six 2x crops were supplied without implementation context. This candidate is not accepted.

High-confidence findings visible in full images and crops:

1. Cabin-side teal lamp has no visible attachment. Add a physical bracket/hanger.
2. Hull reads as a short wheeled platform; raised/flared bow and layered ship form remain too weak. Raise the hull/deck above the wheel crowns, shape the deck edge around the bow and keep the crane/cabin mounted on that deck.
3. Broad surfaces and pulleys read as wood. Inspect atlas mapping and use restrained metal response; retain timber only on the working deck. Material changes require an explicit candidate contract update, not bypassing an old verifier assertion.
4. Crane pulleys are solid lumps. Distinguish sheaves, rims and hubs. The claw's clearance/outward suspension is visually weak; verify geometry and several angles.
5. Wheelhouse damage is too subtle. The breach lies behind the captain's wheel; move the damaged pane into unobscured glazing and inspect its facet attachment.
6. Wheel damage partly reads as ground penetration, with rigid dark fragments. Inspect support and remove unsupported rectangular debris; retain local wheel deformation. The hull's whole-body damage translation also creates a deck gap and needs a local bow dent.

Medium-confidence: a fragment floats by the damaged claw, while cable sag changes little of the overall silhouette. Remove unsupported fragments and retain a broken hoist cable/changed grab pose without toppling the crane shown standing in the art.

The reviewer found substantial improvement over the initial model: recognizable claw, bow light, armored wheels, portholes and roof railing. Root inspection agrees with the defect list; these gains do not excuse the remaining silhouette/material/attachment problems.
