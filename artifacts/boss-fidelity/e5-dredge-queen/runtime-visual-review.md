# Dredge Queen runtime visual review

Current review images: `runtime-final-2/` (20 raw runtime states; six exact full images and six 2x crops reviewed independently). Source and asset hashes are in that directory's report. Both original intact/damage plates were supplied without implementation history. Earlier `runtime-calibration-4` critique exposed clipped labels, claw overlap and an obscured wreck; later frames correct those particular defects. Earlier unsuccessful captures remain historical evidence.

The fresh reviewer finds partial fidelity, not a close 1:1 match. High-confidence residuals: wheels read as small flattened discs at the game angle; the crane/mast/grab silhouette is compressed around the dome; dark hull/claw lose detail against desktop ground; final wreck resembles the half-damaged pose; labels are visually dominant, without attachment cues, and starboard can cover the player. Mobile tuning panel occludes top HUD information. Medium-confidence concern: insufficient contact shadow makes the ship appear suspended. No definite detached component or clipping assembly was found; chain, cabin, mast, hold and wheel guards appear connected. Brass/red cloth/teal and opening hold preserve identity.

Root inspection agrees on compact proportions, weak dark/teal separation, sparse cargo and limited damage distinction. The four original binary damage morphs intentionally remain binary at the existing HP threshold; adding a new death state is outside this focused fidelity correction. Wheel axis is not proven wrong: builder rim endpoints and spokes lie in Blender XZ planes, axle along Y, and the matched neutral V10 comparison shows upright wheels. The actual gameplay camera strongly foreshortens them and hides upper portions beneath covers. The readability complaint remains valid; it is not evidence to rotate wheels horizontally. Larger wheels/taller machinery and stronger scene contrast remain possible subsequent fidelity work.

Current acceptance is limited to an improved, connected and readable asset under calibrated framing. `runtime-final-2` uses actual camera/HUD, hero near the ship and existing mobile maximum zoom-out. It proves neither arbitrary mobile framing nor full encounter save equivalence. The final hulk waits for the normal HUD announcement timer; the earlier story-card-only wait did not clear that independent banner. Both raw and optimized loaders pass 20 actual mesh/morph states; lifecycle regression found later is tracked separately and prevents final handoff until resolved.
