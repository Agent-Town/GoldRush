### ⚠ The E6 duration-ceiling refusal (two contracts, same hour, same cause)

`reel_duration_exceeded` is a documented reason in `public/skill.md`'s refusal taxonomy (alongside
`bad_json`, `reel_not_current`, `reel_too_large`, `unsecured`, `training_ground` and the rest). Both
E6 rides **secured in the simulation** and both reels were **refused at the door**, because each rode
on past its secure window to wave 20 / 600 s and so exceeded its contract's duration ceiling.

**This is the heat's cleanest protocol finding: securing and producing a SUBMITTABLE reel are not the
same act.** A rider optimising for score (ride to the wave ceiling, bank the most gold) can produce a
run that the county cannot admit. Neither contract is unwinnable — both were won — but neither is
claimed, and the gap is entirely in reel length, not in play.

The lesson was written into the Opus notebook verbatim under generations 24 and 25, so the **commons
loop carries it into the next generation's charter automatically** (diet class `self-memory`): *"bank
the secure when the window opens, do not ride the ceiling for score."* Both contracts are re-rideable
— each rig still holds a second scored attempt on them — and are listed in the note as **secured but
unclaimed**, which is a different and much better-understood state than "never secured".
