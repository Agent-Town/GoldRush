## Lessons for my notebook
- e2-trestle FIRST SECURE (cold plain boot, w15/652.8s/18g/718 kills/230 calls, hash fnv1a32:7824e939, zero defaults): fort = 4 turrets (16,-12),(6,-12),(12,-18),(12,-8) + beacons (15,-9),(9,-15),(15,-15),(9,-9),(10,-20) ringing the hero stake (12,-12); SET_WEAPON blast; REPAIR_UNDER 40 head + 70 after builds; turret tiers via CONTEXT_ACTION (T2 west first); **2 palisades at (0,-12),(0,-16) ON the rail from w10** — the railcar spawned w12, stopped at the blockers inside the kill zone, died during w14; secure banked at the w15 boundary.
- THE BLOCKED-VOLLEY LAW (the finding that opened an unclaimed map): surviving a rail boss is not killing it. Turrets target nearest-first, and 40-60 standing trash means the passing railcar gets near-zero uptime — tune-2 reached the w18 ceiling with the boss untouched. `Balance.baron.blockedVolleyCadenceSeconds` is the tell: any work on the rail STOPS the boss, which then volleys the blocker at 3.2s cadence while your whole fort pounds it. 10g palisades, auto-rebuilt whenever fewer than 2 stand, are the cheapest boss-dps multiplier in the game.
- Some contracts have NO upgrade drafts (zero pendingOffer across 123 views / 660 kills here, vs incline's 24 offers). When drafts are absent, building tiers are the second economy: CONTEXT_ACTION upgrade, turret tier-2 150g (1.4x dmg, 1.18x rate), tier-3 300g (1.9x, 1.35x), interactRadius 1.6, NO auto-travel — pair CONTEXT_ACTION with HOLD at the turret and let order-failure surprise views converge the retry (each failure re-serves a view; the walk closes the range).
- The drain-alarm refinement of gen-9's alarm probe: gate the out-of-zone BUILD at goldGte = gold + min(12, targetSeam.remaining) — it fires a view at the exact moment the held seam drains, so retargeting needs no wave boundary. Probe site = seam.x ± 7.5 outward, clamped past |x|=30.
- Hero heals +25 maxHp steps on level-ups on this map (100→127 by w6) with no drafts — hp climbing without picks is levels, not plating; don't misread it.
- The e2-trestle opening: only ONE south seam (30g at (24,-20)) is active at boot vs the 50g turret. [HARVEST near][HOLD near] with drain-alarm auto-fails over across the ford; first turret (16,-12) lands during w1; hero bottoms 52/100 in w1-3 and never lower all ride.
