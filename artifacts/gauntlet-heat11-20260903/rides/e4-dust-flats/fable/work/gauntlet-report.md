## Lessons for my notebook
- e4-dust-flats FIRST SECURE (cold plain boot, w14/444.7s/10g/597 kills/68 calls, hash fnv1a32:cdf4883d, zero defaults): fort = palisades (0,10.5),(-2.5,7) + turrets (0,17),(-11,12.5),(11,12.5),(3,11) + beacons (3,10),(-5,6) around the fixed hero (0,8); rig weapon ALL ride; errand deferred to turrets≥3-or-wave-9 (banked t306); boss died inside wave 14; secure window opened mid-wave and single-element bank took it.
- **SET_WEAPON blast is NOT a universal fixed-hero answer — measure it against the idle floor first.** On this map auto-blast killed 5 in 31s where the naked idle rig killed 34 in 79s (gangs at 1.18× speed STREAM, they don't clump), and my gen-9 "blast for fixed heroes" prior cost me tune-1 entirely. Explicit BLAST_AT at the hero's feet, gated on `blastReadyInMs === 0` and threats≥3, adds the AoE without giving up the rig — one throw per view is plenty and never risks a cooldown view-storm.
- The Land-Yacht (and likely any orbitSpawn boss): components ride the published `orbitSpawn` ring (center, radius, angularSpeed — speed = radius×angularSpeed) with ignoreTerrain, so rail-blocking is void. The counter is an ANNULUS FORT: guns at ring-center distance d∈[8,17] (range 16 reaches the r=24 ring, crane grab reach 6 cannot reach back). Kill wheels first — act 2 pins ALL components where they stand, converting the orbit fight into a stationary one inside your own arc. Act 1 loots fund escorts every 2s (max 4), so beaching fast also caps the escort tax.
- The E4 secure gate is DOUBLE on a baron+motor map: `autoSecureWaveForRun` pins secure while the errand is open, AND `postBaronDefeat` refuses the kill-secure until `motor.objectiveAllowsSecure`. Do the errand before wave 14 or the boss kill is wasted; but do NOT do it first — on a starved economy every pre-boss second of Prospector time is fort gold, and wave 9 is plenty early (55s errand vs boss at ~t460).
- Starved-seam economy signature (3 active seams of 12 anchor slots, 30-50g caps, +10/30s refill, respawn at a DIFFERENT anchor 40-70wu away): sustained income ≈ 0.6-1.0g/s no matter how well you route. Budget the fort to ~300g by wave 13 (2 sponges + 4 turrets + 1-2 beacons, skip everything else) and score seams by d_prospector + 0.7×d_camp so far-corner respawns lose to northern ones.
- Palisade sponges around a fixed hero do NOT tank streaming gangs — mine ended every ride at full hp while the hero bled. They're only worth 20g as pure extra-target insurance; the real hero armor is turret count and tinkers_plating. Rank plating over luck when death, not gold, is the binding wall — but keep double_tap_coil above both on a rig map (+24 hero dps is half a free turret).
- The county floor tape for a shipped era slice (`artifacts/e4-roads-and-convoys/<contract>-floor.tape.json`) is not just an errand playbook — it's a full order-array TRANSCRIPT including the mid-errand re-send pattern (resend only the REMAINING waypoints at each view, or replace-semantics restarts the tour). I copied its jiggle-dwell tar visits (node, node+0.6, node) verbatim and the errand worked first try in every ride that reached it.
