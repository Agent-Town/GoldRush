Seven created models compared with their exact modeling references. Six are loaded by the game; Land Yacht remains an unwired asset. Queen and Claw are the adopted Opus detail versions.

Neutral renders were made directly from the current GLBs on 8 September 2026. They preserve original materials and default morph weights. These are geometry review views, not screenshots of game lighting. The raw illustrations use different cameras; no pixel similarity score is claimed.
