# Baron attempts

- 9 launches: attempt 1 exposed a rider initialization fault; 8 completed deterministic rides. Approximate completed-rider wall: 5 m 20 s.
- Tested home forts, damage and tank upgrade ranks, early turret tiers, early walls, rig-to-Blast switching, four remote turrets, a two-home/two-remote battery, south-bank walls, and a concentrated near-ford wall grid.
- Closest survival was attempt 2 at wave 21. Best measured boss damage was attempt 4: Baron 49,025 HP remaining from roughly 53.3k. The longest fortified attempt (9) survived to 542.700 s but still left 49,164 HP.
- Stopped after the public cold door's full in-run damage envelope remained about 12x short and no new lawful positioning/economy hypothesis remained.
