## Why

Owner ruling 2026-09-07, verbatim in ADR-005:

> "Humans cannot control the positioning of the Prospector, just the rider, for the Prospector they can give "policies" like repair. This has to be 1:1 the same for the AI. There cannot be an unfair advantage here of it being able to control the Prospector like the rider and the human cant."

`rider-parity-audit` measured the whole surface against that law (`docs/bench/rider-parity-audit.md`, 2026-09-07) and found **6 of 18 verbs `agent-only`**. The shape of the advantage is not one verb: a human has ONE freely-positioned body (the hero, with the Prospector drifting behind it and one target-named dispatch to seams and sluices), and a rider has TWO. Every action reach test in GR-SIM reads `this.prospector.position` where the browser reads `this.actionActor.group.position`, so the second body is what the whole context-action family hangs off. Removing `MOVE_TO` alone would leave a rider unable to reach a canal stake, a crater, a vent or a corridor at all.
