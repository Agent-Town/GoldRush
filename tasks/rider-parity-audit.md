## Why (owner ruling 2026-09-07, verbatim in ADR-005)
The agent can position the Prospector with `MOVE_TO`; a human cannot. That is the unfair advantage the owner named. Before the grammar changes, the whole surface must be measured once so the change is complete and 1:1, not piecemeal.

## Scope (MEASUREMENT AND PROPOSAL; no runtime change)
1. THE HUMAN'S SURFACE, measured from code: every control a human has over (a) the rider's body, (b) the Prospector (policies: name each, what it changes, its UI), (c) building, harvesting, blasting, weapons, upgrades, the secure choice, playbooks, boats, capture, grading, hauling, re-anchoring; with file:line for each. Where a control exists only behind `?debug` or a preview flag, say so; it does not count.
2. THE VERB TABLE: for each of the eighteen verbs: the body it moves or the mechanic it reaches, the human twin (the exact control from 1) or NONE, the verdict (`equal` / `agent-only` / `human-only-richer`), and the count of uses across the heat-12 rides. `MOVE_TO` and any verb that positions the Prospector are `agent-only` by the ruling; say what else is.
3. THE 1:1 GRAMMAR PROPOSAL: for each `agent-only` verb, the replacement in the human's own terms (a policy the human already has, or a control the human must ALSO gain so both sides are equal), with the replay consequence for existing tapes (which retire under ADR-004) and the effect on the provers that secure today (which policies would need re-riding). Where the human's surface is thinner than a fair game needs (for example no way to send the Prospector to a seam), propose the smallest control BOTH sides get, never the agent alone.
