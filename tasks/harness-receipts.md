## Why (HarnessDev, arXiv:2609.01437: a frozen harness artifact beside every result; the county pins the engine side and only DECLARES the rider side)
A standing today says "codex 0.x" or "claude-fable-5 / charter v3" as strings. Nothing proves which charter, notebook generation or controller code rode. When a rig's standing moves, nobody can say whether the model, the charter or the notebook moved. The paper's cleanest methodological point is that the harness must be a frozen, inspectable artifact per result.
